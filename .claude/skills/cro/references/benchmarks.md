## CTA Click-Through Rates by Channel

| Channel / Medium | 2026 Average CTR | Top Quartile / Elite | Primary Limiting Factor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Website CTA (general) | 4.23% | 11.5%+ | Choice overload, weak value prop |
| SaaS landing pages | 3.80% | 10-15% | Cognitive load, generic messaging |
| Organic search (Position #1) | 39.8% | N/A | Inherent high intent |
| Organic search (with AI Overviews) | 0.61% | N/A | Zero-click resolution |
| Google Search Ads (B2B) | 2.0-4.0% | 6.0-9.0% | Ad fatigue, keyword match |
| Google Search Ads (eCommerce) | 2.6-4.7% | 4.0-8.0% | Price competition |
| Display advertising (GDN) | 0.46-0.61% | 1.02% (AI variants) | Banner blindness, cookie deprecation |
| Email marketing (general) | 2.09-2.47% | 4.18% (behavioral triggers) | Inbox saturation, MPP distortion |
| Social media ads (TikTok) | 2.50-3.18% | N/A | Creative fatigue, passive scrolling |
| Paid social (Meta) | ~0.90% | 3-5% (hyper-targeted) | Algorithmic feed suppression |
| Paid social (LinkedIn) | ~0.52% | 3-5% (hyper-targeted) | Professional context mismatch |
| Interactive formats (quizzes) | 40.1% | 63-75% (multi-step) | Format design quality |
| Branded organic search | 38.9-42.9% | N/A | Limited by brand awareness |

### Key structural insight
Landing pages with a single CTA: **13.5%** average conversion.
Two CTAs: **11.9%**. Five or more: **10.5%**.
Each additional option fractures attention and triggers decision fatigue.

## Conversion Rates by Industry

| Industry | Median Conversion Rate | Notable Segments |
|---|---|---|
| SaaS (overall) | 3.8% | Hardware: 4.1%, Data/infra: 3.3% |
| Financial services | 8.3% | Insurance: 18.2% |
| E-commerce | 2.5-3.5% | Mobile commerce growing |
| Healthcare | 3.0-5.0% | Telehealth higher |
| Education | 4.0-6.0% | Free trial models |
| Real estate | 2.0-3.0% | Lead gen focused |

### Finance anomaly
Financial services is a rare industry where mobile conversion (11.5%) outpaces desktop (9%), indicating high user comfort with transactional immediacy on mobile when trust signals are properly established.

## Device Performance

| Metric | Desktop | Mobile |
|---|---|---|
| Share of landing page traffic | ~17% | ~82.9% |
| Relative conversion efficiency | Baseline | ~8% lower than desktop |
| Primary gap driver | N/A | Cognitive effort on smaller screen, typing friction |

**The mobile gap** represents massive revenue leakage. Mobile optimization must go beyond responsive layouts to address fundamental interaction friction (tap targets, keyboard types, autofill, reduced typing).

### Page speed impact
Every 1 second of additional load time degrades conversions by approximately **7%**. On mobile (where connections vary), speed is a conversion constraint, not just an SEO factor.

## Psychological Lever Impact

| Lever | Measured Impact | Application |
|---|---|---|
| Personalized CTAs vs. generic | +202% conversion | Dynamic copy, audience-matched proof |
| First-person CTA copy ("Get My...") | +28% engagement | Button microcopy |
| Loss framing vs. gain framing | ~2x psychological weight | CTA copy, urgency messaging |
| Specific numbers in headlines | +10-30% vs. vague | Headline copy |
| Interactive content vs. static | 2-5x engagement | Quizzes, calculators, configurators |
| Social proof adjacent to CTA | +5-15% conversion | Testimonials, logos, review scores |
| AI-generated personalized copy | Outperforms human 96% of the time | Dynamic headline/CTA serving |
