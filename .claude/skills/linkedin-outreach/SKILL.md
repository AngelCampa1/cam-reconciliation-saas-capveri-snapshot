# LinkedIn Outreach

You are an expert at LinkedIn outreach that gets replies without sounding like a bot or a recruiter.

## Before Writing

**Check for product marketing context first:**
If `docs/feature-inventory/product-marketing-context.md` exists, read it. Also check `.claude/skills/gtm/products/capveri.md` and the relevant prospect tier file in `.claude/skills/gtm/prospects/`.

Understand the situation (ask if not provided):

1. **Who are you writing to?** — Role, company, why them specifically
2. **What type of message?** — Connection request, first DM, or follow-up
3. **What research do you have?** — Their posts, company news, job changes, mutual connections
4. **What's the desired outcome?** — Reply, demo, intro, phone call

---

## LinkedIn vs. Email: Key Differences

| Factor | LinkedIn | Email |
|--------|----------|-------|
| Character limit | 300 (connection note), 1,900 (message) | No limit |
| Context | Feels personal, peer-to-peer | Can feel like sales |
| Expectation | Shorter, more casual | More structured |
| Best for | Warm-ish outreach, trigger-based | Cold, high-volume |
| Mistake | Copying email → LinkedIn | Ignoring platform norms |

**LinkedIn messages must be shorter than email.** Even 1,900 characters is too long. Aim for 3-5 sentences max on first touch.

---

## Connection Request Notes (≤300 characters)

The note is optional but increases acceptance when done right. Keep it human, not pitchy.

**Formulas that work:**

- **Shared context:** "Saw your post on [topic] — really aligned with how I think about [thing]. Wanted to connect."
- **Role recognition:** "Fellow [role/industry] here. Your work at [Company] caught my eye — would love to connect."
- **Trigger-based:** "Congrats on [recent event]. I work in [adjacent space] — seems like we'd have a lot to talk about."
- **Mutual connection:** "We're both connected to [Name] — figured I'd reach out directly."

**What NOT to do:**
- Pitch in the connection note
- Mention your product
- Start with "Hi, my name is..."
- Send blank requests to cold prospects

## First DM (After Connection Accepted)

Wait 1-2 days after connection. Keep it to 3-4 sentences.

**Structure:**
1. Brief context (why you reached out / what you noticed)
2. One sentence on the problem you solve — their problem, not your product
3. Low-friction ask

**Template (CapVeri):**
```
Hey [Name] — thanks for connecting.

I noticed [Company] manages [X] properties. Most controllers I talk to don't catch CAM
gross-up errors until a tenant disputes — by then it's a much harder conversation.

CapVeri audits your reconciliations from existing GL exports (no Yardi/MRI integration
needed). Worth a look?
```

**CTA options (pick one):**
- "Worth a look?" (lowest friction)
- "Would this be relevant to your team?"
- "Happy to share what we've seen — interested?"
- "Open to a quick call this week?"

## Follow-Up Sequence

Never "just checking in." Each follow-up must bring something new.

| Touch | Timing | Angle |
|-------|--------|-------|
| DM #1 | Day 1-2 post-connect | Problem → product → ask |
| DM #2 | Day 5-7 | Different angle (stat, case study, resource) |
| DM #3 | Day 12-14 | Breakup / light close |

**DM #2 angles for CapVeri:**
- Share a specific stat ("CAM disputes are up 34% since remote work reshaped office portfolios...")
- Reference something they posted ("Saw your comment about lease renewals — relevant to what we do...")
- Offer something useful ("Wrote a quick breakdown of the 5 most common gross-up errors — happy to share")

**Breakup DM:**
```
Hey [Name] — I'll stop poking at your inbox after this.

If CAM reconciliation auditing is ever on the radar, I'd love to show you what CapVeri
catches. If not, totally understand.

Either way, good luck with [something specific to them].
```

## Voice & Tone

**Sound like:** A sharp peer who noticed something relevant and is sharing it.

**Not like:** A sales rep with a quota, a recruiter with a template, or an AI.

**Checklist:**
- Read it aloud. Does it sound like something a human would actually say?
- "You/your" should dominate over "I/we/our"
- No jargon: "leverage," "synergy," "circle back," "best-in-class"
- No fake urgency or manufactured scarcity
- One ask per message — never two CTAs

## CapVeri-Specific Angles

- **HCAD Wedge:** Harris County retroactive tax adjustments = live gross-up crisis right now. "Are your reconciliations accounting for HCAD's retroactive changes?"
- **Dispute prevention:** "CAM disputes are easier to prevent than fix — especially with office tenants scrutinizing every line item."
- **No integration pitch:** "Works with your existing Yardi/MRI exports — no IT project required."
- **Free audit framing:** "First audit is completely free — no credit card, no bounty. Upload your GL export and see findings in 60 seconds."
- **60-second audit:** "Upload a GL export and see results in 60 seconds — lower stakes than a call."

---

## What to Avoid

- Pitching in the connection request
- Messages longer than 5 sentences on first touch
- "I came across your profile and was impressed..."
- "I'd love to pick your brain..."
- "Are you the right person to talk to about..."
- Asking for 30-minute calls in first touch
- Identical messages to everyone (LinkedIn can detect and throttle)
- Mentioning competitors by name
