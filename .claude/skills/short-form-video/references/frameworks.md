# Short-Form Video Frameworks — CapVeri Reference

## Platform Algorithm Quick Rules

### Instagram Reels
- **1.7-second decision window**: viewer decides to stay or scroll within 1.7s
- If viewer passes 3s → 65% probability they stay to 10s
- <30s = optimized for unconnected/discovery reach
- 30–90s = optimized for follower retention
- >90s = nearly eliminated from recommendation pools
- **Highest signal**: DM shares (sends per reach) > Likes > Comments
- **Rewatch / loop behavior** = secondary booster signal (December 2025 update)

### TikTok
- **Search intent**: algorithm indexes keywords in spoken audio + on-screen text + captions
- Engagement quality > raw views (comments, saves, conversations)
- Off-peak posting → tests in isolated user clusters → velocity in first hour = FYP placement
- **Best for**: Question-Answer-Expansion (QAE) format — state the search query → answer quickly → expand

## Advanced Scripting Formulas

| Formula | Best For | Structure |
|---|---|---|
| **PSB** (Problem-Solution-Benefit) | B2B SaaS, product demos | Pain point → Solution → Downstream benefit |
| **BAB** (Before-After-Bridge) | Transformation stories, case studies | Negative now → Ideal future → How to get there |
| **QAE** (Question-Answer-Expansion) | TikTok SEO, search-intent content | Searched question → Quick answer (≤10s) → Deeper nuance |

## Open Loop Technique (Zeigarnik Effect)

Introduce a compelling premise in the first 3 seconds. **Delay the resolution until the final 5 seconds.** The viewer's brain craves closure — this artificially inflates watch time and completion rate.

**Example for CapVeri:**
> "There are three things landlords put in CAM statements that are completely illegal — and I'll show you the worst one at the end."

[Deliver lesson 1, lesson 2, then...]
> "And that third thing? [Reveal]. If you've seen this in your statement, comment below."

## Creator Methodologies Summary

### Alex Hormozi — High-Density B2B Authority
- **Format**: Talking head, direct to camera (builds parasocial trust)
- **Value Equation**: Value = (Dream Outcome × Likelihood of Achievement) / (Time Delay × Effort)
- Every second must either raise desire or eliminate friction
- **Visual style**: Bold uppercase captions synced to speech, color hierarchy (yellow/green for action words), emojis as anchors
- Dynamic captions increase retention by 42% (50% of viewers watch on mute)

### MrBeast — Retention Editing
- **"ADHD Editing"**: ruthlessly cut any frame that doesn't advance the narrative
- No dead air — every second provides a dopamine hit or pattern interrupt
- **CTR is downstream of satisfaction**: great previous video → automatic next-video click
- One video with 10,000 views > ten videos with 1,000 views each

### Alix Earle — Parasocial Continuity Loop
- "If you watched my last video, you know…" → rewards loyal viewers, creates FOMO for newcomers
- New viewers binge previous content to get context → inflates total watch time per user
- **Applied to CapVeri**: Running series ("The CAM Audit Files, Episode 3") builds returning audience

### Ali Abdaal — Evergreen Architecture
- Prioritize **evergreen topics** (perennial search traffic) over trends
- Script long-form → extract 5-minute micro-lessons for Reels/TikTok
- Outsource editing to scale output without burning out

## Pattern Interrupts (Sustaining Mid-Video Attention)

Human attention resets when exposed to novel stimuli. Use **multi-sensory jolts** every 7–10 seconds:

- **Visual**: Camera zoom, B-roll cutaway, animated text pop-up, color change
- **Auditory**: Sound effect, music transition, silence break
- **Sequencing**: Text pop at 1.0s → sound effect at 1.5s → camera zoom at 2.0s (layered, not simultaneous — simultaneous overloads)

## Audio Strategy (2026)

**Hybrid approach performs best**: high-quality original voiceover (primary) + subtle trending track at low volume (secondary).
- Trending audio alone is increasingly deprioritized by the algorithm
- Original authoritative voiceovers + trending background = discoverability + uniqueness
- "Shrinking song" trend: artists front-load hooks in first 15–30 seconds for easy clipping

## Accessibility = Algorithmic SEO

- Accurate captions: +38% retention, +65% completion rate
- Captions feed text to OCR → amplifies video's SEO footprint on both platforms
- Font: clean sans-serif (Arial, Montserrat, Poppins) — no decorative fonts
- Background contrast ratio ≥4.5:1

## Formats to Avoid in 2026

- Physical camera-cover or arm-swipe transitions (perceived as amateurish)
- Flashing valuable content on screen for a fraction of a second (hostile bait)
- Pre-recorded content passed off as live webinars
- Overused 2024 slang in professional contexts ("demure", "delulu")
- AI-only, uncustomized avatars with no original voiceover
