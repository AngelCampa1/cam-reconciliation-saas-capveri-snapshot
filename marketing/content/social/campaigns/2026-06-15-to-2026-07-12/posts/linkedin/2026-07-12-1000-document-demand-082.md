A good CAM packet answers this before anyone asks:

Confirm what the lease allows before sending files.

The response should track lease audit rights, record scope, redactions, delivery, and follow-up.

A messy response can make a normal review feel adversarial.

Control note: prove the input before the output.

The fix is boring. Save the support near the math.

Keep the response factual and organized.
