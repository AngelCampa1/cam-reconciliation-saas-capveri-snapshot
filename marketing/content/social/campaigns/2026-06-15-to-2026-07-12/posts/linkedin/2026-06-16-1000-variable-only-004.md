Why it matters: Variable expenses can be grossed up. Fixed costs need separate treatment.

Sort taxes, insurance, utilities, janitorial, and service costs before applying a factor.

Grossing up fixed costs can overstate the recovery pool.

The packet should show the fixed and variable split.
