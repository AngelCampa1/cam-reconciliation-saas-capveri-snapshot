Let's define "CAM season" precisely, because it means something different to different teams.

For some property management organizations, CAM season is a discrete period: January through March, during which the prior year's reconciliation is produced and distributed to tenants.

For others, it's a rolling condition. Properties on non-calendar fiscal years, multi-tenant portfolios with staggered lease anniversaries, and properties that were acquired mid-year all require reconciliation work that doesn't align to a single window.

The "season" framing matters because it shapes how teams staff and prioritize. A team built for a concentrated January-March sprint handles rolling reconciliation differently than a team that processes it year-round.

A few questions worth thinking about: Does your team's staffing model match the actual cadence of your portfolio's reconciliation cycle? Are there properties that get less attention because they fall outside the peak window? Is there a single person who holds the institutional knowledge for each property's CAM history?

Not asking for answers in the comments, just useful prompts for a Friday afternoon.
