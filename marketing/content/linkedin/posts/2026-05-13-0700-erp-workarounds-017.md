Six fields in Yardi's recovery pool setup drive 90% of CAM calculation errors. Most controllers have inherited their Yardi setup from a predecessor and modified it incrementally. That's how the errors accumulate.

Field 1: GL Account Mapping. New expense accounts added during the year don't automatically join the pool. At year-end, compare your total GL operating expenses to your recovery pool total. Any gap is unmapped accounts.

Field 2: Occupancy Type. Physical vs. Economic is not a minor difference. A building that ran at 88% for ten months and dropped to 75% when a large tenant vacated in November shows 75% at December 31. Using that snapshot inflates the gross-up adjustment. it doesn't reflect what the building actually cost to operate for the full year.

Field 3: Gross-Up Threshold. The pool-level threshold may not match every tenant's lease. Tenant A says 95%. Tenant B says 90%. Tenant C has no gross-up clause. A single pool setting cannot handle all three correctly without tenant-level overrides.

Field 4: Variable vs. Fixed Classification. Property taxes are fixed. Insurance premiums are fixed. Contract-based landscaping is fixed. When these get flagged as Variable, they get grossed up. And Tenants get charged for an inflation in expenses that doesn't actually happen when occupancy improves.

Field 5: Pro-Rata Denominator. Tenant RSF divided by the wrong denominator changes every tenant's share. The denominator must match the lease definition: total building RSF, leaseable area, or occupied area.

Field 6: Cap Configuration. Non-cumulative versus cumulative produces dramatically different results over multi-year periods. After a lease renewal where the cap resets, the base year usually needs to be updated manually. That update doesn't happen automatically.

Check these six fields before every reconciliation cycle. Most of the "Yardi is wrong" escalations trace back to one of them.
