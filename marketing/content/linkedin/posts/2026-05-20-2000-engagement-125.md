A definition worth being precise about: "configuration drift" in CAM reconciliation.

Configuration drift is when the settings in your ERP no longer match what the executed lease requires. The lease was amended. The renewal changed the cap structure. A modification removed an exclusion. The ERP wasn't updated to reflect the change.

This is different from a calculation error. The math in the ERP is running correctly. The inputs to the math are wrong.

It's also different from negligence. Configuration drift usually happens because the lease amendment process and the ERP configuration process are handled by different people with different workflows and no formal handoff between them.

The fix isn't better accountants. It's a verification step that explicitly compares what's in the lease to what's in the ERP, every reconciliation cycle.

Most portfolios have at least some degree of configuration drift active right now. The question is how long it's been running and how much it's accumulated.
