"A CAM expense per square foot that is 20% above BOMA or IREM benchmarks for the property type is not necessarily an overbilling. But It is a question tenants will ask. The landlord who can answer it with documentation wins the conversation. The landlord who can't starts a dispute."

Portfolio-level benchmarking identifies outliers before reconciliation season. Building-level investigation finds out why they're outliers. Sometimes it's market conditions. Sometimes it's an expense that shouldn't be in the pool.
