Six fields in the Yardi recovery pool setup control 90% of the CAM calculation. None of them default to the right value for your leases.

Most Yardi setups were configured during an initial implementation and then inherited. The person who built the configuration may not work there anymore. The leases have been renewed, amended, and expanded. The configuration has not.

Field 1: GL account mapping. New expense accounts added mid-year after a chart restructuring never make it into the pool. The expense exists in the GL but never reaches the tenant statement.

Field 2: Occupancy type. Physical occupancy is a point-in-time snapshot. A building at 85% for ten months and 70% for two months shows 70% physical occupancy at December 31. Economic occupancy (weighted average) for the same year is 82.5%. Using the snapshot inflates the gross-up.

Field 3: Gross-up threshold. The pool is set to 95%. Tenant B's lease says 90%. Tenant C's lease has no gross-up clause at all. A single pool-level threshold applied to all tenants does not match three different leases.

Field 4: Variable versus fixed classification. Property taxes are fixed. Insurance is fixed. When these are flagged as variable and the building has vacancy, they get grossed up. This is an overcharge.

Field 5: Pro-rata share denominator. The lease says total building RSF. Yardi is using occupied RSF. Every tenant's share percentage is wrong.

Field 6: Cap type. Non-cumulative applied when the lease says cumulative. Year 1 is identical. Year 2 diverges. By Year 4, the discrepancy is significant and the prior statements need to be restated.

Six fields. If any one is wrong, every statement from that pool is wrong.
