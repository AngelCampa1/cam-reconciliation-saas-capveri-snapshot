Hook line (say on camera, first 3 seconds):
"The reason CAM errors survive for years isn't that anyone is being careless. It's that there's no built-in step that checks the configuration against the lease."

Talking points:
- Property accountants know the formulas. Gross-up, pro-rata, cap application. The issue is inputs, not formulas.
- The ERP was configured when the lease was executed. Leases get amended. The ERP configuration usually doesn't follow.
- Common examples: gross-up threshold changed in an amendment, exclusion added in a modification, cap type changed from non-cumulative to cumulative in Year 3.
- These are silent errors. The system runs clean. The report looks normal. The statement goes out.
- The fix is an independent calculation step: read the current lease, run the numbers from scratch, compare to the ERP output.
- That comparison is the step that's missing in most reconciliation workflows.
- CapVeri runs that step from a CSV export of your GL. No ERP integration needed.

Closing line:
"The lease is the contract. The calculation should start from the lease, not from what the ERP was told three years ago."
