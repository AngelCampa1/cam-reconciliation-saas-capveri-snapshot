SLIDE 1:
Moving off Excel for CAM reconciliation.
What to migrate, what to skip, and the one data point you cannot afford to lose.

SLIDE 2:
When to do it
Not during reconciliation season. Full stop.
Migrate in Q2 or Q3, after year-end close, before the next cycle begins.
Budget 7-12 business days and 60-108 hours of actual work for a 10-property portfolio.

SLIDE 3:
What you're actually migrating
GL data: don't migrate from spreadsheets. Pull fresh exports from your PM system. Your spreadsheets contain a processed version of this data, which may include errors you'd be importing.
Lease terms: tenant RSF, pro-rata denominator, gross-up threshold, cap provisions, base year amounts. 15-30 min per tenant from a clean abstract.

SLIDE 4:
Historical reconciliation results
What to migrate: prior year total recoverable expenses, prior year tenant billings, prior year cap bank balances, prior year base year amounts.
What not to migrate: intermediate calculation cells, embedded notes, conditional formatting, macros and VBA code.
You want inputs and outputs. The formulas in between get replaced by the new system.

SLIDE 5:
The critical data point: cumulative cap bank balances
If any leases have cumulative CAM caps, the bank balance is the one data point that cannot be reconstructed from GL exports alone.
It exists only in your spreadsheet, or your controller's memory.
If it's not migrated, the bank resets to zero and you lose the ability to recover those banked amounts permanently.

SLIDE 6:
Reconstruct the bank from first principles
Go back to the first year the cap provision took effect.
For each year: cap allowance minus actual increase.
Sum positive differences (years where actual was below cap).
Subtract draws (years where banked capacity was used).
Document the reconstruction. Save it alongside the migrated data.

SLIDE 7:
Common migration pitfalls
Migrating errors: running the new system's validation on imported historical data catches prior-year problems before they propagate.
Skipping the parallel reconciliation: run one property through both old and new systems, compare tenant by tenant. "The numbers look right" is not validation.
Trying to migrate everything at once: batches of 5-10 properties, validated before the next batch starts.

SLIDE 8:
First property migration: under 4 hours.
10-property portfolio: typically live within two weeks.
The transition only needs to happen once.
