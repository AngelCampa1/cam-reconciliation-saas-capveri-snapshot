A Yardi or MRI GL export, one CSV upload, and CapVeri runs an independent CAM reconciliation against your lease terms, surfacing every gross-up, cap, or exclusion error before statements go out.

You export your GL from whatever system you're using: Yardi, MRI, AppFolio, or Excel. You upload that CSV. CapVeri runs an independent CAM reconciliation calculation against your tenant lease terms, using BOMA 2024 standards.

It then compares its output to the figures in your GL. Anywhere there's a variance, it surfaces the finding with the specific tenant, the specific calculation element, the dollar amount, and what the lease says should have been applied.

No ERP integration. No API credentials. No IT department involved. One CSV file.

The output is a reconciliation report your property accountants can act on directly.
