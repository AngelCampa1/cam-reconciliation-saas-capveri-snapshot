Hook line (say on camera, first 3 seconds):
"Three management fee errors. One of them is technically circular math."

Talking points:
- The first error: using the management agreement base instead of the lease base. These two documents often define the fee base differently. The lease controls what's recoverable from tenants. If the management agreement says "5% of gross revenues". The lease says "3% of recoverable CAM expenses," you cannot bill 5%. - The second error: the circularity trap. If the fee is a percentage of the CAM pool, and the management fee is itself in the CAM pool, the formula is: Fee = Rate × (Pool + Fee). Solving for Fee gives you: Fee = Rate × Base / (1 - Rate). At 15%, that's $88,235 on a $500,000 pool, not $75,000. Most billing systems calculate $75,000 and leave $13,235 uncollected every year. - The third error: billing both a management fee and an admin fee when the lease allows only one. The management fee covers the PM company. The admin fee is a separate markup the landlord charges on top of operating expenses. Some leases permit both. Most permit one. Check the lease clause, not the property management agreement. - All three errors are invisible until someone reads the lease against the GL. Yardi will calculate whatever you configured. It won't flag the mismatch.

Closing line:
"The lease controls. The PM agreement doesn't."
