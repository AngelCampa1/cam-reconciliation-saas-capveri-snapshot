SLIDE 1:
Why commercial landlords lose 3-5% of CAM recoveries every year
(And what they're usually not checking)

SLIDE 2:
The problem is not the ERP
Yardi and MRI run the numbers correctly.
The problem is that the numbers they're given are wrong.

SLIDE 3:
Where configuration drift starts
A lease amendment changes the gross-up threshold.
The ERP still uses the old one.
Two years pass. No one notices.

SLIDE 4:
The cap calculation that compounds quietly
Cumulative caps, base-year anomalies, year-over-year resets.
Each one is a separate setting in the ERP.
Each one can drift from the actual lease language.

SLIDE 5:
The exclusion list problem
Tenants negotiate exclusions (janitorial, management fees, capital items).
The ERP includes everything unless explicitly removed.
Most portfolios have at least one inclusion that should be excluded.

SLIDE 6:
The pro-rata denominator
Rentable vs. usable area. BOMA 2024 vs. older measurement standards.
A denominator set in 2019 from a floor plan that was remeasured in 2022.
That error multiplies across every tenant on the floor.

SLIDE 7:
The fix is not a new ERP
The fix is an independent calculation layer that reads the lease directly.
Compare ERP output vs. lease-based output. Find the delta. Fix the source.
