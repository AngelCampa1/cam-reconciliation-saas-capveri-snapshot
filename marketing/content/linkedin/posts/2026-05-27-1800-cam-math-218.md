SHORT CAPTION:
Base years set the financial floor for every reconciliation the tenant receives over the lease term. A 2020 or 2021 base year is one of the most consequential terms anyone can sign. And Most tenants don't model it at signing. Curious what practitioners are actually seeing in the field.

QUESTION: What base year situation do you encounter most often in your portfolio?

OPTION A: Pandemic-era base years (2020-2021) still producing inflated-looking true-up charges
OPTION B: Base years that were never grossed up to a stabilized occupancy level
OPTION C: Renewal options that reset the base but the ERP was never updated
OPTION D: Base year leases that were never reconciled because "it never matters until it does"
