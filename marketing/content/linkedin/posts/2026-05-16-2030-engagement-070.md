Four years of signed CAM statements, no dispute. Year 5, the tenant hires an auditor. The exclusion was in the lease exhibit from day one: management fees above 3% of gross revenues.

The tenant has occupied the space for four years. Every year, they've paid the CAM reconciliation without dispute. Year 5, they hire an auditor before paying.

The auditor finds an exclusion that was in the lease exhibit from day one: management fees above 3% of gross revenues. The ERP was configured to include all management fees with no cap.

Four years of overcharges. The tenant is now asking for credits going back to Year 1.

The landlord's property accountant ran correct calculations. The ERP ran correctly. The lease exhibit didn't match the ERP configuration, and no one compared the two until Year 5.

This isn't unusual. It's the normal outcome when lease-term verification isn't built into the annual reconciliation process.

What process does your team use to verify that what's configured in the ERP still matches the lease?
