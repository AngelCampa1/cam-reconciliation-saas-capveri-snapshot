Up to 3.5% of operating expense recoveries by property type, based on industry estimates derived from tenant audit firm published findings and operating-expense benchmarks.

That's the share of operating expense recoveries that commercial landlords miss each year, mostly to calculation errors and reconciliation work that falls through the cracks.

On a portfolio with $2 million in annual CAM billings, that's $60,000 to $100,000 in recoveries that were owed but never collected.

The individual errors are usually small. A gross-up factor applied when occupancy was already above threshold. An exclusion that was in the lease but not in the ERP. A cap type set to non-cumulative when the lease says cumulative. No single item is alarming. But they compound across tenants and across years.

This is what motivated Angel Campa to build CapVeri. Not because property accountants are doing bad work, but because the system they're working in doesn't check its own configuration against the lease.

The ERP calculates what it was told to calculate. Someone has to check whether it was told the right thing.
