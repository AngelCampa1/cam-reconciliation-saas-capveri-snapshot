"Gross-up applied to fixed expenses like property taxes and insurance consistently ranks as one of the most common findings in tenant audit engagements, with dollar impacts that typically range from $20,000 to $150,000 per occurrence depending on building size and recovery pool."

Fixed expenses don't vary with occupancy. Grossing them up inflates tenant charges in every below-threshold year. It's the first thing every tenant auditor checks, and it's the error most property managers have never been explicitly trained to avoid.
