Yardi Voyager CAM errors almost never come from one random misconfiguration. They come from one of five layers. in a specific order.

Recovery Pool. Charge Code. Lease Setup. Billing Setup. Manual Overrides.

That's the sequence. A correct output at each layer depends on the layer above being correct. This is why checking Layer 3 when the error is in Layer 1 wastes hours.

Start at the pool. Every tenant in a pool inherits pool-level errors. If your gross-up threshold is wrong at the pool level, every tenant on that pool gets the wrong number. And Fixing individual lease records won't change the outcome until the pool is right first.

The charge code check is faster than most people expect: open the charge code used for CAM billing and confirm the Recovery Code field is populated and points to the correct pool. A blank field here means the charge code cannot pull from pool data at all. The tenant gets billed, but from nowhere.

Lease setup errors are usually denominator problems. The pro-rata fraction in Yardi should match tenant RSF divided by whatever denominator the lease specifies. If Yardi shows 5.26% and the lease says 5.00%, the field is using occupied area instead of total building RSF.

Manual overrides are the wild card. They exist for legitimate reasons. They also survive lease renewals when they shouldn't. Before concluding anything is correct, check whether an active override is superseding the rest of the configuration.

Fix top-down. That order alone saves hours of chasing symptoms that trace back to the wrong layer.
