SLIDE 1:
The no-integration idea behind CapVeri
Why we don't connect to your ERP (and why that's the right call)

SLIDE 2:
The integration objection is real
Every ERP vendor has a different API. Authentication requirements change. Version upgrades break connections. Your IT team has to maintain them.
Most property management teams can't afford that overhead.

SLIDE 3:
The CSV is already there
Yardi, MRI, AppFolio, RealPage. Every major ERP produces a standard GL export.
Your property accountant already pulls this report for year-end close. No IT department needed.

SLIDE 4:
A CSV has no attack surface
No OAuth tokens. No API credentials stored in a third-party system. No dependency on the ERP vendor's uptime or security posture.
A file is a file. You control when it's uploaded.

SLIDE 5:
The calculation layer runs independently
CapVeri doesn't rely on what the ERP calculated.
It reads your GL data and recalculates from the lease terms, using BOMA 2024 standards.
That independence is what allows it to find configuration errors.

SLIDE 6:
Configuration drift is what causes most CAM errors
The ERP calculates what it was told. The lease changed. The ERP wasn't updated.
An independent calculation from the lease catches that drift.
An ERP integration can't catch it, because it inherits the same configuration.

SLIDE 7:
What stays the same
Yardi. MRI. Your GL structure. Your accounting team. Your year-end workflow.
CapVeri adds one step: upload the CSV, compare the output, fix what's off.

SLIDE 8:
No implementation consultant. No API contract. No IT project.
One CSV. One comparison report.
