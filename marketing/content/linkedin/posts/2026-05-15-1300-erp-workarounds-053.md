RealPage's CAM pool configuration wizard walks you through setup step by step. That makes it easy to get started. And Easy to accept defaults that don't match your leases.

The defaults most likely to cause problems:

Cap type defaults to non-cumulative. Many commercial office leases from 2015 onward specify cumulative caps, where unused headroom carries forward. If the pool is set to non-cumulative. The lease says cumulative, tenants in years with low CAM growth have banked capacity they'll never receive credit for. Over a five-year lease with a 5% cumulative cap and actual increases of 3%, 3%, 3%, 8%, 8% the five-year billing difference between cumulative and non-cumulative can exceed $15,000 on $100,000 annual CAM.

Cap base year doesn't update automatically at renewal. When a lease renews and the CAM cap resets, RealPage requires a manual update to the base year in the lease record. If the base year still points to the original lease commencement, cap calculations are wrong for the entire renewal term.

No cap rules configured at all. This is the single most common RealPage overbilling scenario. The pool calculates an uncapped amount and sends it to tenants who have cap protection in their leases. Always configure cap rules for any lease that contains CAM cap language.

Mixed-use account contamination. Properties with both residential and commercial tenants in the same RealPage database risk residential-specific GL accounts being mapped to commercial CAM pools. amenity costs, apartment maintenance, residential common area expenses. An account numbering convention that separates residential and commercial from the start prevents this.

Anchor tenant denominator treatment. Major tenants often negotiate excluded-from-denominator status. If RealPage has them contributing to the denominator when the lease says they shouldn't, every other tenant in the building is undercharged.

The validation checklist before statements go out: cap type verified, cap base year current, GL accounts reviewed, allocation basis confirmed against lease definitions. That checklist catches most RealPage overbilling before it becomes a dispute.
