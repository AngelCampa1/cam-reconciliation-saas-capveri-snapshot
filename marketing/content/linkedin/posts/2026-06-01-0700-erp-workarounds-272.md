RealPage CAM exports often need cleanup before they can support independent reconciliation.

Four structural issues create the most risk: embedded subtotal rows, multi-line descriptions, inconsistent date formats, and blank separator rows.

Three reports usually do most of the work:

- Recovery Reconciliation from Commercial > Recovery Analysis
- GL Detail from Accounting > General Ledger > Transaction Detail
- Rent Roll from Commercial > Lease Reporting

The Recovery Reconciliation shows actual expenses versus estimates by tenant and expense pool. It is useful, but it is only as correct as the configuration behind it.

Cleanup step 1: embedded subtotal rows.
RealPage can insert subtotal rows within account groups, tenant sections, and periods. Those rows look like data and can double-count expenses if imported as transactions.

Cleanup step 2: multi-line descriptions.
Some transaction descriptions spill into continuation rows. A parser needs to merge them into the correct transaction record.

Cleanup step 3: date formatting.
One report may use MM/DD/YYYY, another YYYY-MM-DD, and another text dates. Normalize dates before joining reports.

Cleanup step 4: blank separator rows.
Blank rows are visual structure in Excel but empty records in a CSV pipeline. Remove them before import.

After cleanup, verify the recovery configuration: GL account mapping, gross-up threshold and occupancy method, cap settings, excluded tenants, and allocation method.

The cleanup work is front-loaded. The configuration verification is the part that requires lease-level judgment.
