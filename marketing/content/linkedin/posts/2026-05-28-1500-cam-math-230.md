SLIDE 1:
12 checks before the reconciliation statement goes out
Most disputes come from skipping one of these.

SLIDE 2:
Check 1: GL exclusion scrub
Remove CapEx and other non-recoverable items before any calculation runs. CapEx inclusions are among the largest preventable dispute triggers.

SLIDE 3:
Check 2: Variable vs. fixed classification
Gross-up applies to variable expenses. Applying it to property taxes or insurance creates a dispute that is easy for tenants to find.

SLIDE 4:
Check 4: Pro-rata denominator reconciliation
Confirm the RSF basis matches the BOMA standard or fixed denominator in each lease. A wrong denominator creates systemic variance.

SLIDE 5:
Check 6: Pro-rata shares sum to 100% or below
If tenant shares exceed 100%, you may have double collection. If they are far below 100%, you may have leakage.

SLIDE 6:
Check 8: Cap structure verification
Confirm cumulative versus non-cumulative against the signed lease. The wrong cap type compounds across the lease term.

SLIDE 7:
Check 10: Management fee and admin markup audit
One fee. One calculation base. No corporate overhead in the pool. Confirm the clause before statements go out.

SLIDE 8:
Check 11: Vendor invoice completeness
A December invoice posted in February cannot always be back-billed. Chase AP stragglers before the statement closes.
