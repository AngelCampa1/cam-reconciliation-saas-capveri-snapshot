"Tenant auditors find material discrepancies in the majority of reconciliation statements they review. The errors are not random. they concentrate in seven categories that have been consistent for decades: CapEx coded as OpEx, gross-up on fixed expenses, management fee overstatement, expenses outside the lease definition, pro-rata share errors, cap violations, and base year anomalies."

Every one of those categories is checkable before the statement is sent. The finding rate persists because the checks aren't being run, not because the errors are hard to find.
