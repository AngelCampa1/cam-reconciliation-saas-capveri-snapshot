SLIDE 1:
You don't need to replace Yardi to automate CAM reconciliation.
Here's the workflow that actually works.

SLIDE 2:
The core problem
Yardi calculates what you told it to calculate.
It has no knowledge of what your leases actually say.
When a lease changes and the configuration doesn't, the error runs silently for years.

SLIDE 3:
Step 1: Export your GL as CSV
Yardi's standard GL detail export. No API. No credentials. No IT department.
The same report your property accountant already runs for year-end close.

SLIDE 4:
Step 2: Apply lease terms independently
An independent layer reads the raw GL and recalculates from scratch.
It doesn't inherit Yardi's recovery pool configuration.
It starts from the actual lease: gross-up threshold, cap type, pro-rata denominator, exclusion list.

SLIDE 5:
Step 3: Compare outputs
Every difference is flagged with:
- The specific tenant
- The specific calculation element (gross-up, cap, pro-rata share)
- The dollar amount of the discrepancy
- The lease clause that supports the correct number

SLIDE 6:
What stays the same
Your Yardi instance. Your GL structure. Your IT setup. Your existing workflow.
Zero integrations. Zero API credentials. Zero implementation project.

SLIDE 7:
What changes
You find configuration drift before statements go out, not after tenants dispute.
Q1 reconciliation shifts from building calculations to reviewing flagged discrepancies.

SLIDE 8:
Before: 6-8 weeks of Q1 reconciliation work, 3-4 controllers calculating from scratch.
After: 1-2 weeks of focused review, errors caught pre-statement.
The savings come from eliminating manual calculation. Not from replacing the ERP.
