"The ERP didn't make a mistake. It calculated exactly what it was configured to calculate. The lease said something different."

This is the sentence that property accountants hear most often when a tenant audit comes back with findings.

The gap between what the ERP is configured to do and what the lease actually requires is where most CAM recovery leakage lives. It doesn't show up in the system. It shows up in the dispute.

What's your team's current process for catching that gap before statements go out?
