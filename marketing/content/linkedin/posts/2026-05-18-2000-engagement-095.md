A tenant who quietly pays five years of CAM reconciliations without a word, then hires an auditor in Year 6, is reviewing all five years simultaneously: every gross-up factor, every exclusion, every cap misapplication, compounded. The tenants who never dispute aren't always the safe ones.

That five-year tenant is more problematic than one who disputes small items annually. The five-year tenant has been building a case.

The tenant who disputes annually may be more work in the short term. But the disputes also force your team to verify the calculations. That verification catches errors early, while they're small and relatively easy to resolve.

Dispute frequency is not a great proxy for reconciliation quality. Quiet tenants can be a lagging indicator.

Has this played out at your properties, or does the pattern look different?
