Generating CAM estimate adjustment letters manually takes 15 minutes per tenant. For a 60-tenant portfolio: 15 hours, or nearly two full business days.

The contrarian point: the time cost is not the main problem. The error rate is.

When you copy numbers from a reconciliation workbook to a Word template 60 times in a row, transposition errors are inevitable. Tenant A gets Tenant B's estimate. The monthly amount doesn't match the annual total divided by 12. Suite 400 shows Suite 300's rate.

Each error requires a correction letter, an apology call, and 30 minutes of rework. On a 60-tenant portfolio, expect 3 to 5 correction letters. That's 2 to 2.5 additional hours. plus the trust damage from sending wrong numbers to tenants who are already scrutinizing CAM charges.

The systematic fix doesn't require software. It requires structure: a single data source where all estimate numbers live, a template that pulls from that source rather than being typed, and a validation step that confirms the monthly amount × 12 = the annual total before any letter is finalized.

For anyone still doing this manually: pull the current estimate and new estimate for every tenant into a single spreadsheet first. Do the math check in the spreadsheet. Then generate from that verified source. The correction letter you don't send is worth more than the hour you save on generation.
