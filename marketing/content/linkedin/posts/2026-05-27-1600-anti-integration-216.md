For a 30-building portfolio, the CAM reconciliation timeline comparison:

Excel process: 8 weeks, 4 controllers, errors discovered by tenant auditors after statements go out.

Independent verification layer: 3 weeks, same 4 controllers, errors caught before statements go out.

The same staff. The same ERP. The same GL export they already produce. What changes is that a purpose-built system reads the export, recalculates from lease terms, and flags every discrepancy before the statement is generated.

The 5-week time reduction is meaningful. The shift from post-statement to pre-statement error detection is the part that actually changes the financial exposure. A $15,000 gross-up error caught before the statement costs an hour to correct. The same error caught by a tenant's auditor costs the original credit or refund, 20-40% to the auditor on contingency,. A relationship that does not fully recover.

Excel doesn't have a validation layer. It calculates whatever formulas you give it. It does not know whether those formulas match your leases. It cannot tell you when a lease renewal changed the gross-up threshold and the formula was never updated.

Independent reconciliation software does not replace the ERP. It reads the ERP's export and checks the math against the lease. Where the two diverge, the discrepancy appears. before anyone outside your team sees the number.
