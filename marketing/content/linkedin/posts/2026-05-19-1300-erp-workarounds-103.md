A Yardi export that "looks right" still fails reconciliation. Here is why.

Yardi exports fail in the setup layer, not the math layer.

The four most common causes: the report ran in summary mode instead of detail mode, posting-date filters cut off December accruals posted in January, recovery pool mappings are stale after a mid-year chart of accounts change, or the Excel file still contains header rows and subtotals that break any downstream processing.

The three exports that cover a complete CAM reconciliation: Recovery Analysis (what Yardi thinks each tenant owes, by pool), General Ledger Analytics (the expense detail feeding those pools), and Rent Roll with Lease Charges (tenant roster, square footage, lease timing).

Skip any of the first three and the validation is incomplete.

The QA sequence that saves time: compare GL totals to the same-period financial statements first. Then confirm Recovery Analysis is detail-level and pool-complete. Then confirm the rent roll has the expected tenant roster and square footage. Only then inspect gross-up, caps, or pro-rata math.

That order is faster than jumping straight into formulas.

One check catches most export failures before they become reconciliation variances: verify that every GL account with a non-zero balance appears in the recovery pool. Any account with a balance that is not in the pool is a potential gap.

The export is not done until every item on that checklist is confirmed.
