Ranking the 15 most common CAM billing errors by frequency misses the point for most property managers.

Frequency tells you which errors are most likely to exist in a given portfolio. It doesn't tell you which errors are worth the most to fix, which errors generate the most tenant disputes, or which errors are hardest to catch without systematic review.

The error that scores highest on all three dimensions is gross-up applied to fixed expenses. It is consistently the most common finding in tenant audit engagements. It generates more tenant dispute letters than any other single cause. And it requires a deliberate check to catch. the billing system will apply whatever pool definition it receives,. It will not flag that property taxes are in the variable category.

Dollar impact: $20,000 to $150,000 per occurrence, depending on pool size and how many years the error has been running.

The next two errors. management fee calculated on the wrong base and CapEx coded as OpEx. are close behind in frequency and dollar impact. Both require looking at the lease to catch. The billing system produces a valid-looking output regardless of whether the inputs are correct.

What the ranking of 15 errors actually shows is that the errors property managers encounter most often are not random. They concentrate in three places: gross-up calculations, expense categorization, and pro-rata share calculations. Those three areas are where systematic review is worth the most.

Running the full 15-item check is due diligence. Running the top three catches the majority of what auditors find.
