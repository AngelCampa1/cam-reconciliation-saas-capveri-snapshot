A working definition we use at CapVeri: "CRE FinOps" is the discipline of making lease-based financial recoveries as traceable and auditable as a line-item invoice.

Most financial operations frameworks are built around spending visibility: what you're paying, to whom, and whether it's justified. CRE FinOps is the recovery-side equivalent. What you're owed, under which lease, calculated by which formula, and collected against which GL entries.

The gap in commercial real estate is that the spending side (GL management, accounts payable) is well-instrumented. The recovery side (CAM reconciliation, lease-based billing) often runs on a combination of ERP configuration and institutional knowledge, with no independent verification layer.

CRE FinOps as a practice asks: is every recovery you're entitled to under your executed leases being captured, calculated correctly, and billed? If not, where is the gap and what created it?

That's the problem CapVeri is built to address. The term is useful because it frames reconciliation accuracy as an ongoing operational discipline rather than an annual event.
