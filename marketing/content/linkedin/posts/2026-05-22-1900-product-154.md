The CAM audit software category is mostly built around two approaches: consulting engagements and ERP add-ons.

Consulting is accurate but expensive and episodic. It happens when someone decides to pay for an audit, not every reconciliation cycle.

ERP add-ons extend the system you already have, but they inherit its configuration. If the configuration is wrong, the add-on runs the same wrong calculation faster.

CapVeri is neither. It runs an independent calculation from your lease terms using a CSV export of your GL. No connection to your ERP. No inherited configuration. The output is a comparison: what CapVeri calculated from the lease versus what your GL reflects.

Configuration drift, which is the most common source of CAM errors, shows up in the comparison. The ERP add-on doesn't see it because it's running from the same configuration. An independent layer starts fresh from the lease.
