SLIDE 1:
What AI can and cannot do in CAM reconciliation.
The line that separates useful from dangerous.

SLIDE 2:
The one question that clarifies everything
Is the AI doing extraction, or calculation?
Extraction: fast, useful, appropriate for AI.
Calculation: must be deterministic, reproducible, auditable.
The consequences of confusing the two are not theoretical.

SLIDE 3:
Where AI adds real value: document extraction
A 180-page commercial lease contains the CAM provisions you need.
Manually abstracting it takes 2-4 hours for an experienced paralegal.
AI extraction cuts that to 15-30 minutes of review time.
A 12-property portfolio generates roughly 3,600 vendor invoices per year. 480 hours of manual data entry becomes 120 hours of review.

SLIDE 4:
Where AI adds real value: GL classification and anomaly detection
GL classification maps Yardi account codes to standardized expense categories.
It handles 90% of entries and flags the 10% that need human judgment.
Anomaly detection finds year-over-year expense spikes, duplicate invoices, and seasonal breaks. in a dataset too large for manual review.

SLIDE 5:
Where AI creates risk: financial math
A gross-up on a $2.1M expense pool at 73.4% occupancy with a 95% cap has exactly one correct answer.
A deterministic engine produces that number every time.
An AI model may produce a different number. And Cannot show its work.
"The AI calculated it" does not survive a deposition.

SLIDE 6:
The other hard limits
Lease interpretation: AI extracts the clause. A human decides what it means.
Sign-off authority: the decision to release statements involves legal interpretation and business judgment. No AI system should authorize that release.

SLIDE 7:
The right architecture
Stage 1. AI Extraction: reads leases, invoices, GL exports, classifies codes.
Stage 2. Human Verification: every extracted value confirmed before calculation.
Stage 3. Deterministic Calculation: Python engine, exact Decimal arithmetic, same output every time.
Stage 4. Audit Trail Storage: every step retrievable three years from now.

SLIDE 8:
Three questions to ask any "AI-powered CAM" vendor
Does the AI perform the calculation, or just the extraction?
Is there a human verification step before data enters the calculation?
Can you export the full calculation ledger. every input and every intermediate step?
