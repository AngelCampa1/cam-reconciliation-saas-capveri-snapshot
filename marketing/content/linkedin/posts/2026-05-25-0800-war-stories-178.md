"A CPA verifying CAM charges for a tenant runs seven independent checks. GL reconciliation, expense categorization, gross-up verification, denominator check, cap application, exclusion list review, and final tie-out. The process takes 4-6 hours per lease. Property managers who run the same checks before the statement goes out remove the financial case for the audit."
