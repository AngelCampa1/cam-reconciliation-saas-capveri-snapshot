SLIDE 1:
Your CAM numbers don't match Yardi.
5 root causes. And How to fix each one.

SLIDE 2:
Root Cause 1: Miscoded AP Invoices
Yardi runs accurate math on whatever's in the GL.
Non-recoverable expenses coded to recoverable accounts get billed to tenants automatically.
The fix: GL scrub before reconciliation runs. Pull every invoice in recoverable accounts and verify against the lease's expense exclusion list.

SLIDE 3:
Root Cause 2: Unapplied Cash
Payments received but not matched to a charge code float as unallocated balances.
They distort CAM pool totals by amounts that look like partial payments.
The fix: Run a full unapplied cash report before year-end close. Match every open balance to its charge.

SLIDE 4:
Root Cause 3: Square Footage Frozen at Lease Execution
Yardi defaults to the SF recorded when the lease was signed.
Re-measured buildings, BOMA updates, space reconfigurations, none of these update automatically.
The fix: Compare SF in Yardi against current measurement for each tenant. Any discrepancy needs manual update before the pro-rata calculation runs.

SLIDE 5:
Root Cause 4: Gross-Up Applied to the Full Expense Pool
Yardi's out-of-box gross-up may apply the multiplier to all expenses. not just variable ones.
A $50,000 property tax bill grossed up at 50% occupancy adds $50,000 in phantom charges. Taxes don't double when the building fills up.
The fix: Separate fixed and variable expenses. Apply the multiplier only to the variable pool.

SLIDE 6:
Root Cause 5: Custom Cap Logic Not Configured
Cumulative cap banks, compounding structures, per-tenant cap schedules. these are not Yardi out-of-the-box functionality.
G2 reviews of Yardi Commercial Management note that executing custom lease caveats requires extensive, costly support.
The fix: For every tenant with a cap clause, verify that Yardi has been configured and tested for that specific cap structure.

SLIDE 7:
What all five have in common:
Yardi didn't fail. It calculated what you configured.
The discrepancy is between configuration and what the lease actually requires.

SLIDE 8:
An independent calculation layer reads your GL export and applies lease terms directly. not Yardi's configuration.
That's how you find these gaps before a tenant auditor does.
