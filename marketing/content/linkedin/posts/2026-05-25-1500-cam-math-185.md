You don't need to review every GL transaction before the reconciliation. You need to review the right ones.

A 200,000 SF building produces 3,000 to 5,000 operating expense transactions per year. Full review: 8 to 12 hours. Most controllers don't have that during reconciliation season.

The 30-minute version catches 80% of dollar errors:

Minutes 1-5: Pull every transaction over $10,000 from operating expense accounts. For each one, ask: recurring operating expense or one-time capital item? A $45,000 "HVAC compressor replacement" in the HVAC maintenance account is likely CapEx. A $4,500 quarterly service call is OpEx. Same account number, completely different nature.

Minutes 5-10: Compare each expense category to prior year. Flag anything that moved more than 15%. The HVAC line at 55% is the most common signal. a capital item coded to operating.

Minutes 10-20: Check management fees against the lease-permitted cap. Verify insurance and tax amounts against the actual invoices. Check for duplicate vendor payments in the same account. these show up in portfolios managed by multiple teams.

Minutes 20-30: Document your findings. Items that cleared the review get noted. Items that need investigation get flagged before the reconciliation runs, not after statements are drafted.

Thirty minutes at the front prevents eight hours of rework at the back.
