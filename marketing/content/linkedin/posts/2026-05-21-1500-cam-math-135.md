SLIDE 1:
The CAM reconciliation checklist
(25 steps. Three of them account for most of the disputes.)

SLIDE 2:
Phase 1: Data Collection (Steps 1-6)
Pull the GL export, confirm the reconciliation period, pull the rent roll, pull signed lease abstracts, identify prior-year adjustments, collect insurance and tax bills.
The starting point matters: use raw GL. don't filter before you verify exclusions.

SLIDE 3:
Phase 2: Expense Pool Preparation (Steps 7-13)
Categorize inclusions and exclusions. Remove CapEx. Remove management fee overage above the lease cap. Split controllable vs. non-controllable. Verify the denominator. Compute each tenant's pro-rata share. Adjust for partial-year tenants.

SLIDE 4:
The step most people skip: Step 11. denominator verification.
Your denominator is not the building's total SF. It's the lease-defined rentable area, excluding any anchor space, co-exclusions, or special carve-outs.
On a 500,000 SF property where five tenants exclude their space, the effective denominator might be 420,000 SF. Using 500,000 overbills every other tenant.

SLIDE 5:
Phase 3: Adjustments (Steps 14-18)
Apply gross-up if occupancy was below the lease threshold. Example: $180,000 in variable expenses at 80% occupancy grossed to 95% = $213,750.
Apply CAM caps. base year, fixed percentage, or cumulative. Compute the carryforward bank for cumulative caps. Reconcile against estimated payments.

SLIDE 6:
Step 14 (gross-up) and Step 17 (cap carryforward) are where most errors concentrate.
Gross-up error: applying the formula to fixed expenses, not just variable ones. Fixed costs don't vary with occupancy.
Cap carryforward error: losing the cumulative schedule. Landlords who lose it under-recover in every low-increase year after the fact.

SLIDE 7:
Phase 4: Review and QC (Steps 19-22)
Run variance analysis vs. prior year. Cross-check total pool against GL totals. Verify the statement format includes all required components. Pull the contractual delivery deadline from each lease.
No statement should go out without Step 22 confirming the delivery window is open.

SLIDE 8:
Phase 5: Delivery and Follow-Up (Steps 23-25)
Generate the full reconciliation package (statement + GL backup + cap worksheet + gross-up worksheet + denominator documentation). Deliver with timestamp confirmation. Track settlement payments with a 30-day receivables target.
Three steps. Three failure points. Run the checklist sequentially every time.
