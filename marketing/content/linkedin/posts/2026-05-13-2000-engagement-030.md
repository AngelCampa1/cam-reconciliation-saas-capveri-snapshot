Gross-up applied to $420,000 in fixed expenses, cap type misread for 11 tenants. Neither dispute was about the formula. Most CAM disputes are not really disputes about the math.

They're disputes about what the lease actually says.

The tenant's auditor isn't usually arguing that multiplication was done wrong. They're arguing that the gross-up factor shouldn't have been applied at all, or that a specific expense category was excluded in the lease exhibit but included in the pool, or that the base year was defined differently in the lease than what the ERP was configured with.

The math ran correctly. The inputs were wrong.

This matters because it changes what the fix looks like. Better Excel templates don't solve a problem that lives in the gap between lease language and ERP configuration. The fix requires reading the lease and comparing it to the system, line by line, before statements go out.

Agree, or have you seen it play out differently?
