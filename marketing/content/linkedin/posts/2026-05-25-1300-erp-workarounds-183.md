Hook line (say on camera, first 3 seconds):
"Your CAM numbers don't match Yardi. It is almost never a Yardi bug."

Talking points:
- Yardi runs accurate math on whatever lives in the GL. That is the problem. If the inputs are wrong, the output is wrong, and Yardi has no way to flag it. - Five root causes account for almost all Yardi CAM mismatches. First: miscoded AP invoices. Non-recoverable expenses coded to recoverable accounts. marketing spend, leasing commissions, CapEx. get included in the CAM pool. The fix is a GL scrub before the reconciliation runs. - Second: unapplied cash. Tenant payments received but not matched to a specific charge code sit as unallocated balances. Distort pool totals. Reconcile the cash ledger before year-end close. - Third: square footage frozen at lease execution. Yardi defaults to the SF recorded when the lease was signed. Re-measurement after a renovation or BOMA standard update requires a manual update before the pro-rata calculation runs. - Fourth: gross-up applied to the full expense pool instead of variable expenses only. Property taxes, insurance, and contract-based services do not change with occupancy. A $50,000 tax bill grossed up at 50% occupancy produces $100,000 in charges. Tenants' auditors check this first. - Fifth: cap logic that was never configured. Cumulative cap banks and compounding cap structures require specific paid configuration in Yardi. Teams that assume the system is handling cap math it was never set up to do will overbill tenants every year the cap should have applied. - The diagnostic sequence: GL scrub first, then unapplied cash, then SF verification, then gross-up base review, then cap configuration check. Only after those five steps should you move into lease-level calculation comparison.

Closing line:
"Five inputs. If any one is wrong, the output is wrong. Fix the inputs."
