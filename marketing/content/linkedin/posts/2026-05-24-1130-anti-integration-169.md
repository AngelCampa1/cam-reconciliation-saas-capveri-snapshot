SLIDE 1:
CAM automation for 20-50 building portfolios.
Too large for Excel. Not large enough for enterprise implementation.
Here is the middle path.

SLIDE 2:
The mid-market position
Too large for Excel: manually reconciling 30 properties produces inconsistent results and creates audit trail problems when disputes arise.
Too small for enterprise: full ERP replacement runs $500K-$2M and takes 12-18 months. For a 30-building portfolio, implementation cost may exceed multiple years of savings.
The answer is not a replacement. It is a verification layer.

SLIDE 3:
Five failure points in multi-property CAM
Inconsistent denominators: different properties use different definitions. total RSF, occupied RSF, or custom.
Recovery pool drift: new GL accounts added but not mapped, cap base years not updated at renewal.
Gross-up threshold inconsistencies: different leases, different thresholds, pool-level defaults applied universally.
Cap calculation errors: cumulative vs. non-cumulative logic mismatched to lease language.
GL close timing: reconciliation built on preliminary data before final adjustments post.

SLIDE 4:
What standardization actually means
Not the same spreadsheet template.
The same validation steps, calculation methodology, and documentation format applied to every property.
When a process is standardized, a junior analyst executes it correctly because the process enforces the right decisions. the analyst does not need to remember them.

SLIDE 5:
The right automation stack
Layer 1: ERP (Yardi, MRI, RealPage) handles lease administration, billing, and GL management. Keep it.
Layer 2: Independent verification receives GL exports from the ERP, recalculates every tenant's reconciliation from lease terms and GL data, and flags discrepancies between the independent calculation and ERP output.
These two layers serve different purposes. The ERP calculates based on configuration. The verification layer confirms the configuration matches the lease.

SLIDE 6:
90-day implementation for 30 buildings
Days 1-30: onboard two pilot properties (your most complex and simplest). Run independent calculation against prior year reconciliation. Document discrepancies, these are existing errors in the portfolio.
Days 31-60: onboard remaining properties in batches of 5-8. Build the standardized GL export protocol.
Days 61-90: run the current-year reconciliation cycle. All statements reviewed before delivery.

SLIDE 7:
ROI for a 30-building portfolio
Recovered unmapped expenses (3% of $400K average pool): $360,000/year.
Avoided overbilling liability: $150,000/year.
Staff time savings (15 hours x 30 properties x $85/hour): $38,250/year.
Tenant dispute resolution savings: $25,000/year.
Total estimated annual value: $573,250.

SLIDE 8:
The verification layer catches configuration drift before it reaches tenants.
The ERP stays. The spreadsheets retire.
