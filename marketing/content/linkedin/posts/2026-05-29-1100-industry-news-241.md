SLIDE 1:
How to benchmark operating expenses against BOMA and IREM
(And what to do when your building is an outlier)

SLIDE 2:
Why benchmarking matters
You cannot catch every expense classification error by reading the lease alone.
Benchmarking tells you which line items look anomalous compared to similar buildings.
Outliers need investigation. Some have legitimate explanations. Some have GL problems.

SLIDE 3:
The two primary sources
BOMA Experience Exchange Report (EER): office, retail, industrial. Organized by building class, size, and market.
IREM Income/Expense Analysis: multifamily and commercial. Similar segmentation.
Both are annual publications. Use the most recent edition for the current reconciliation.

SLIDE 4:
How to segment for a valid comparison
Match your building's class (A/B/C), type (office/retail/industrial/MOB), and market (urban/suburban/metro).
A Class A urban office should not be benchmarked against Class B suburban office.
The comparison is only meaningful within the same segment.

SLIDE 5:
Mapping GL categories to benchmark categories
BOMA and IREM use standardized expense categories. Your GL may not.
Map each GL account to the benchmark category before comparing.
"Maintenance" in your GL may span two or three benchmark categories. Split it.

SLIDE 6:
What to do with outliers
Above-benchmark: investigate for expenses that don't belong in the pool.
Below-benchmark: investigate for CAM leakage. recoverable expenses coded to non-pool accounts.
Neither is automatically an error. Both require a lease-to-GL comparison to determine root cause.

SLIDE 7:
Multi-year trend analysis
A single year above benchmark may be a genuine market event.
Two consecutive years above benchmark without a market explanation is a systematic issue.
Track year-over-year trends in addition to absolute comparisons.

SLIDE 8:
Benchmarking is a diagnostic, not a defense.
A building above BOMA benchmarks is not automatically overcharging tenants.
But "we're within BOMA benchmarks" is a much easier conversation to have than "we can't explain the variance."
Run the comparison before reconciliation season. Investigate before tenants ask.
