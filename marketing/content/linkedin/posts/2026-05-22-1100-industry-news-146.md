SLIDE 1:
CAM reconciliation deadlines
The timeline most property managers discover too late

SLIDE 2:
The standard window
Most commercial leases require reconciliation statements within 90-120 days after year-end.
For calendar-year leases, that means March 31 to April 30 is the typical due date.
Missing the deadline can void the landlord's right to collect a final true-up.

SLIDE 3:
The practical window is shorter than it looks
The March-April statement deadline assumes the GL is closed, the expense pool is verified, and the documentation is ready.
Most property management operations close the GL in January. That leaves 60-90 days.
Two months to catch errors that have been accumulating for 12.

SLIDE 4:
Documentation readiness must precede statement delivery
Sending a statement you cannot support is worse than a late statement in most markets.
If the tenant requests backup documentation and it isn't available, the dispute starts immediately.
Build the documentation package before the statement goes out.

SLIDE 5:
The audit window runs in the other direction
Most leases give tenants 12-36 months after receiving the statement to initiate an audit.
Some leases extend this to 48 months.
A statement issued in March 2026 may be under audit in early 2029.
The documentation needs to survive that long.

SLIDE 6:
March and April are not the time to find problems
The reconciliation process should be running in parallel with the GL close. not starting after.
Expense pool review, gross-up check, denominator verification, and cap history confirmation should be complete before the statement is drafted.

SLIDE 7:
What gets missed when the process starts too late
Gross-up errors discovered after the statement is sent require restatements.
Cap bank errors discovered after delivery require amended statements and, sometimes, retroactive credits.
Expense classification errors discovered in June are disputes starting in June.

SLIDE 8:
The deadline is fixed. The preparation window is not.
Start the reconciliation review in January. Identify errors in February.
Deliver a statement in March that you can defend in 2029.
