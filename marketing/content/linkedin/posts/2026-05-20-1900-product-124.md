The assumption we push back on: a reconciliation tool should plug into your ERP.

The problem with that assumption is that an ERP integration inherits the ERP's configuration. If the gross-up threshold was set incorrectly three years ago, a connected tool will run that incorrect threshold just as faithfully as the ERP does.

The only way to find configuration errors is to run an independent calculation from the lease terms themselves, then compare it to what the ERP produced.

That's what CapVeri does. The calculation starts from your lease data, not from your ERP's settings. The comparison shows where the two diverge.

You can see what that output looks like before committing to anything.
