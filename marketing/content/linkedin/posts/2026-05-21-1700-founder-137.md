From Angel Campa, CapVeri founder:

"Early in scoping this product, I spent time looking at what types of CAM errors show up most consistently across tenant audit findings.

The expectation going in was that errors would be concentrated in complex lease structures: large portfolios, sophisticated tenants with detailed negotiated exclusions, unusual BOMA methodology variations.

That's not what the pattern showed.

The errors that appear most consistently are in plain-vanilla situations. Standard gross-up clauses. Basic cap structures. Common exclusion language. The complexity wasn't the problem. The problem was that no one had compared the ERP configuration to the lease recently.

A straightforward 95% gross-up threshold is simple to configure correctly. It's also simple to leave set at the wrong threshold for four years if there's no annual step that checks it.

This changed how we thought about the product. The value isn't in handling unusual cases. The value is in doing the routine check, every cycle, without relying on someone remembering to do it manually."
