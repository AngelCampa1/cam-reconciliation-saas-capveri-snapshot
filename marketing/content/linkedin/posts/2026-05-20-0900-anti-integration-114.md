"We can't afford to replace Excel for CAM reconciliation right now."

This assumes the current cost is zero.

It is not.

Excel calculates whatever formula you give it. When a lease renews and the gross-up threshold changes from 95% to 90% but the spreadsheet is not updated, Excel continues calculating at 95%. The error is invisible. The statement looks professional. It goes out wrong.

Version control is the second failure mode. At Q1 crunch, three controllers are working simultaneously across 30 buildings. One has v4_FINAL. One has v5_FINAL_corrected. One has whatever they downloaded last Tuesday. Someone will generate statements from the wrong version.

The third failure: when the controller who built the master spreadsheet in 2019 leaves, the institutional knowledge leaves with them. The formulas work until they don't, with no documentation of why the unusual ones are there.

Replacing Excel does not mean replacing the ERP. The ERP handles GL management and billing. Independent reconciliation software reads the ERP's export and validates calculations against lease terms. No integration, no implementation project.

For a 30-building portfolio, the comparison is 8 weeks with high error rate discovered post-statement versus 3 weeks with errors caught pre-statement.

The cost of "free" is the error exposure, the disputes, the staff time responding to tenant auditors, and the credit and refund cycle that follows. For most portfolios above five properties, that exceeds the cost of purpose-built software in year one.
