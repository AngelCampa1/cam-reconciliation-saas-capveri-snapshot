"The tenant who never disputes is not necessarily the tenant who agrees with your calculations."

It's a useful reminder heading into renewal season.

Long-term tenants who have been quiet on CAM for several years are sometimes building a file. When the renewal conversation starts, the CAM history becomes a negotiating point.

Running an independent lease-vs-ERP check before the renewal discussion, rather than after, gives you the stronger position in that conversation. You either confirm that the reconciliation history is clean, or you find and correct the errors before they become negotiating pressure.

What does your team do to prepare the reconciliation record before a major tenant renewal?
