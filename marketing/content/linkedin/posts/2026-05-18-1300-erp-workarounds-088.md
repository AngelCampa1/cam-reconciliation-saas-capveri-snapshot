Hook line (say on camera, first 3 seconds):
"You don't need to replace Yardi to fix your CAM reconciliation."

Talking points:
- Most teams searching for a "Yardi alternative" don't want to replace Yardi. They want to trust their CAM output.
- Replacing Yardi requires data migration, 12-18 months of implementation, retraining, and $500K-$2M+ for a mid-market portfolio. A replacement ERP has the same CAM configuration problem, just with different settings screens.
- The ERP isn't the problem. The lack of independent verification is the problem.
- What you actually need: a way to verify Yardi's output before statements go out, something that catches configuration drift, and an audit trail that holds up when tenants challenge the reconciliation.
- None of those require replacing Yardi. They require a layer that sits alongside Yardi.
- The workflow: export GL from Yardi (standard report, same one you run for month-end close), upload to independent reconciliation software, compare outputs. Where Yardi's calculation diverges from the lease-term calculation, you see the specific discrepancy with the lease clause that governs it.
- Setup time: one afternoon per property, not 18 months.

Closing line:
"Fix the verification layer. Keep the ERP."
