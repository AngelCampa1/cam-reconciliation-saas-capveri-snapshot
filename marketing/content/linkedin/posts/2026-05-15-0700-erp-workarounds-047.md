The Share Type field in MRI may be the single most consequential configuration choice in CAM setup. And it's frequently set incorrectly at initial property entry, then never revisited.

Here's what it controls:

Pro-rata share = Tenant RSF / Share Type Denominator.

Building Share Type uses total building RSF as the denominator. Floor Share Type uses only the RSF of the floor where the tenant is located.

Same numerator. Completely different denominator. Completely different bill.

A concrete example: 120,000 RSF building. Tenant on Floor 3 occupies 15,000 SF. Floor 3 total is 30,000 SF.

Under Building Share Type: 15,000 / 120,000 = 12.5% of a $600,000 pool = $75,000 charged.
Under Floor Share Type: 15,000 / 30,000 = 50% of a $80,000 Floor 3 operating cost pool = $40,000 charged.

The correct answer depends entirely on what the lease says. Not which number is larger.

Language to look for in the lease: "Tenant's proportionate share of the Building's operating expenses" points to Building level. "Costs attributable to the floor on which the Premises are located" points to Floor level.

The audit process is straightforward. Export the tenant roster from MRI with Share Type, Floor, and RSF columns. Pull lease abstracts. For each tenant, confirm the MRI Share Type matches the lease denominator definition. Spot-check three to five tenants with manual calculations.

Pay special attention to tenants who moved in during the past three years, tenants who renewed with amended CAM terms, and multi-floor tenants. these are where the Share Type is most likely to be mismatched.
