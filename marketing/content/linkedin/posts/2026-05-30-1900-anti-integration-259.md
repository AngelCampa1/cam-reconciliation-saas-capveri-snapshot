SLIDE 1:
Industry benchmark for CAM reconciliation: 90-120 days after year-end.
Most portfolios don't hit it.
Here is what the delay actually costs.

SLIDE 2:
The financial exposure of a slow reconciliation
$100,000 in NOI = approximately $1-2 million in asset value at market cap rates.
A reconciliation that takes 6 months instead of 3 delays that cash flow by a quarter.
The delay is not neutral. it is a real carrying cost on real money.

SLIDE 3:
Legal exposure: California SB 1103
California's SB 1103 imposes an 18-month deadline for delivering reconciliation statements to qualified commercial tenants.
Miss the deadline and the right to collect that year's reconciliation may be forfeited.
SB 1103 is California-specific, but similar audit window provisions exist in commercial leases across all markets.

SLIDE 4:
The tenant audit window problem
Most commercial leases give tenants a 12-36 month window to audit reconciliation statements after delivery.
The audit window starts on delivery date.
A statement delivered in October for a January-December reconciliation year gives tenant auditors nearly to the following October to find errors.
A statement delivered in April gives them 6 months less exposure time.

SLIDE 5:
The accuracy-speed connection
The business case for purpose-built reconciliation tools is well documented. Savings come from both corrected billing gaps and avoided dispute costs.
Speed and accuracy are the same problem. slow reconciliation is usually also inaccurate reconciliation.

SLIDE 6:
Why reconciliation takes so long
65-80% of cycle time is waiting and rework, not calculation.
Waiting for AP to confirm invoice allocations.
Reworking statements after a discrepancy is found post-draft.
Manually verifying that formulas match lease terms.
The calculation itself is a small fraction of total cycle time.

SLIDE 7:
What compresses the cycle
Pre-close GL classification finalized before year-end.
Independent verification run before any draft statements are generated.
Batch delivery rather than drip statements.
Each stage starts only when the prior stage is confirmed accurate.
The goal is to remove rework, not to rush the process.

SLIDE 8:
90-120 days is the benchmark.
Getting there requires removing the rework cycles, not working faster.
