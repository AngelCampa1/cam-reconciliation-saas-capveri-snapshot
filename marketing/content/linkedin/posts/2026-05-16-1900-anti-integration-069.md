SLIDE 1:
Mid-market PMC CAM automation.
The right approach for 20-50 buildings, without an enterprise implementation.

SLIDE 2:
The problem at this scale
Too large for Excel: inconsistent results, version control failures, no audit trail.
Too small for enterprise implementation: $500K-$2M+ costs, 12-18 months, disrupts reconciliation season.
The middle path: standardized verification on top of your existing ERP.

SLIDE 3:
The 5 failure points in multi-property CAM
1. Inconsistent denominator tracking across properties
2. Recovery pool configuration drift (silent, accumulates over years)
3. Gross-up threshold inconsistencies across tenants
4. Cap calculation errors (cumulative vs. non-cumulative mismatch)
5. GL close timing variability: reconciliations built on preliminary data require rework

SLIDE 4:
What standardization actually means
Not the same spreadsheet template.
Same validation steps. Same calculation methodology. Same documentation format.
A process that enforces the right decisions, so a junior analyst can execute it correctly.

SLIDE 5:
The two-layer stack
Layer 1: Your ERP (Yardi, MRI, RealPage) handles lease admin, rent collection, AP/AR, billing.
Layer 2: Independent verification reads GL exports, recalculates from lease terms, flags discrepancies.
The ERP calculates based on configuration. The verification layer checks whether that output matches the lease.

SLIDE 6:
ROI for a 30-building portfolio
The following is a modeled illustrative estimate; actual results vary by portfolio size, lease complexity, and current configuration accuracy:
Recovered unmapped expenses (3% of pool): $360,000/year
Avoided overbilling liability: $150,000/year
Staff time savings (15 hrs × 30 properties × $85/hr): $38,250/year
Tenant dispute savings: $25,000/year
Total estimated annual value: $573,250
(Modeled estimate. Inputs: industry-estimate operating-expense recovery gap ranges; tenant audit firm published contingency rates.)

SLIDE 7:
Implementation: 90 days, not 18 months
Days 1-30: Pilot two properties. Find existing errors in your portfolio.
Days 31-60: Expand in batches of 5-8. Build internal reconciliation checklist.
Days 61-90: Run the full reconciliation cycle. All statements reviewed before delivery.

SLIDE 8:
The core insight
Your ERP is not the problem.
The lack of independent verification is the problem.
Fix the verification layer. Keep the ERP.
