Hook (0-3 seconds):
"MRI calculated your CAM reconciliation correctly. The problem is what it was told to calculate."

Talking points:

Point 1. Expense Group Coverage Gaps
MRI's Expense Group defines which GL accounts are in the recovery pool.
On a 180,000 SF office building with $298,500 in annual operating expenses, leaving janitorial, security, and landscaping out of the Expense Group costs $53,700 per year in unrecovered expenses.
Tenants owe that money. MRI just wasn't told to bill for it.

Point 2. Allocation Method Mismatch
MRI supports multiple allocation methods: pro-rata by RSF, by occupancy, and by fixed percentage.
A $420,000 expense pool split between a 15,000 SF tenant and a 10,000 SF tenant:
Pro-rata by RSF produces $252,000 and $168,000.
Fixed percentage produces $231,000 and $189,000.
A $21,000 difference for the same tenant. which method is correct depends entirely on what the lease says.
MRI applies whatever method is configured. It does not verify the configuration against the lease.

Point 3. Recovery Parameters
Gross-up threshold. Variable/fixed classification. Occupancy period (calendar year vs. lease year).
Each parameter must match the lease for each tenant.
Misconfigured recovery parameters produce wrong numbers that look like right numbers. the calculation completes without errors.

Point 4. Charge Code Linking
In MRI, the chain runs: Expense Group → Recovery Pool → Charge Code → Lease Recovery → Tenant Billing.
A break anywhere in that chain means a tenant who owes recovery never gets billed for it.
No error message. The chain simply doesn't produce a charge.

Closing:
MRI's CAM calculation engine is sound. The errors that produce material discrepancies are configuration errors. settings that don't match leases, expense groups that weren't updated when the chart of accounts changed, allocation methods set at implementation and never reviewed. The way to find them is to compare MRI's output to an independent recalculation from the same GL data. That comparison is what shows you where the configuration diverged from the lease.
