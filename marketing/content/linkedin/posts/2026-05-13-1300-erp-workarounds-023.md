On $500,000 of variable expenses, the difference between Physical and Economic occupancy in Yardi can be $82,500 in grossed-up pool total. billed directly to remaining tenants.

Physical occupancy: snapshot at December 31. If a large tenant vacated in November, December 31 shows 15 points lower occupancy than the building's weighted average for the full year.

Economic occupancy: weighted average across the entire period. Accounts for every move-in and move-out date.

One field in Recovery Pool Setup. Outsized financial impact when buildings have mid-year tenancy changes.
