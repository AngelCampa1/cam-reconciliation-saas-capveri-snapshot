HOOK (0:00-0:08):
CAM expense caps have a 4-step verification process that most portfolios skip. Here's what you check and why it prevents the most recoverable landlord error in CAM.

TALKING POINT 1 (0:09-0:28):
Step one: confirm the cap type from the signed lease. Cumulative or non-cumulative. These two produce different ceilings whenever actual expenses come in below the cap in any prior year. Most ERPs have this as a dropdown. Most of the time, no one checks it against the lease after the original setup.

TALKING POINT 2 (0:29-0:50):
Step two: confirm the base year and base year amount. For a non-cumulative cap, the base is the prior year actual. For a cumulative cap, the base compounds from the original base year. Example: $140,000 base year, 5% cap, year 4. Cumulative ceiling: $140,000 × 1.05^4 = $170,137. Non-cumulative ceiling: prior year actual × 1.05. a completely different number if prior years came in below the ceiling.

TALKING POINT 3 (0:51-1:10):
Step three: confirm which expenses are subject to the cap. Caps apply to controllable expenses. Property taxes and insurance are almost always excluded. Some leases also exclude management fees. The controllable pool you apply the cap to must match the lease's definition. not the system's default configuration.

TALKING POINT 4 (1:11-1:30):
Step four: for cumulative caps, confirm the carryforward bank balance. Every year that actual expenses come in below the cumulative ceiling, the landlord banks unused capacity. That bank allows recovery above the stated rate in high-expense years. Landlords who lose the bank schedule lose the recovery. Build the ledger per tenant. Keep it updated annually.

CLOSING (1:31-1:45):
Four checks. Any one of them wrong produces a ceiling error that compounds across every remaining year of the lease. The most expensive is losing the cumulative carryforward. because it's silent until expenses spike and the landlord bills less than they're entitled to.

ON-SCREEN TEXT:
Step 1: Cap type (cumulative vs. non-cumulative) verify against lease
Step 2: Base year + base year amount. drives every ceiling calculation
Step 3: Controllable expenses only. confirm which accounts are inside the cap
Step 4: Carryforward bank. cumulative caps only, required for accurate ceiling in spike years
