"A 12% property tax increase on a building where taxes are 35% of the CAM pool moves total tenant charges by over 4% before any other expense changes."

Property taxes are a focal point in major-metro reconciliations for 2026. Harris Central Appraisal District is in the process of issuing 2026 notices, and Cook County's south and west suburbs are under triennial reassessment for the 2026-2028 cycle. The aggregate increases will not be final until the official notice cycles close, but property controllers should be pulling the actual 2026 assessment values for each building before the gross-up calculation locks in.

For property controllers, this means the single largest line in the CAM pool is under scrutiny this reconciliation season. Tenants will ask questions. The cover letter and supporting documentation you send with the statement determine whether they accept the bill or call an auditor.
