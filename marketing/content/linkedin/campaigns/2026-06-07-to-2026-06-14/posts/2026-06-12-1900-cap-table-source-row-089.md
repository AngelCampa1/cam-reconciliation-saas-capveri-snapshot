A cap table is weaker when the numbers are anonymous.

Each row needs a source.

Prior-year controllable expenses from the reconciliation.
Current-year actual controllable expenses from the GL and classification file.
Cap percentage from the lease.
Carry-forward rule from the lease.
Bank balance from the prior cap schedule.
Amount billed from the statement.

That makes the cap table slower to build.

Good.

Fast cap schedules are how old assumptions survive.

For cumulative caps, the bank balance is only useful if the source trail survives with it.
