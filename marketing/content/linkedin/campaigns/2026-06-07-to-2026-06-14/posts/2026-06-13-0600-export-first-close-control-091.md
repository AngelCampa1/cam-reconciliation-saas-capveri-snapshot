A CAM close does not need a new ERP project to get cleaner.

It needs the export set to tie.

For a Yardi workflow, the practical control is simple enough to inspect:

Recovery Analysis
GL Analytics
Rent Roll with Lease Charges

Same property. Same period. Detail-level output. Flat files without report headers or subtotals mixed into the data.

If those three files do not agree, formulas are a distraction. The reconciliation may still print, but the support package is already weak.

CapVeri is built around that export-first reality. Keep the system of record where it is. Pull the files that already exist. Test the reconciliation before the statement leaves the building.

That is a better place to start than another integration roadmap.
