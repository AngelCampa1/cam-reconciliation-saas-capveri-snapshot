A cumulative CAM cap needs memory.

That is the part spreadsheets lose.

If actual controllable expenses come in below the cap ceiling, unused room may carry forward when the lease allows it. If expenses spike later, that bank can change the effective ceiling.

Under a non-cumulative cap, the unused room disappears.

Same base year. Same cap rate. Different recovery path.

The control is not complicated:

Calculate the annual cap ceiling.
Compare actuals to the ceiling.
Add unused room or draw from the bank.
Keep the bank from going negative.
Carry the balance into the next year.

The hard part is preserving that trail across fiscal years, template changes, ownership changes, and staff turnover.

Cap math breaks when history gets treated like a note in last year's workbook.
