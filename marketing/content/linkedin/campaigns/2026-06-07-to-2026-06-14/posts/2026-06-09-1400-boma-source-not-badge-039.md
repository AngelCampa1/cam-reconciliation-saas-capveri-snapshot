BOMA 2024 is a measurement input, not a marketing badge.

For CAM, the practical question is simple:

Did the rentable square footage used in the reconciliation change?

If yes, the packet needs the source:

Old measurement.
New measurement.
Lease language that allows or blocks the update.
Effective date.
Tenant-level pro-rata impact.

CapVeri can work with BOMA-referenced measurement inputs.

That does not mean a certification, endorsement, or shortcut around the lease.

Measurement changes belong in the backup, not in someone's inbox after billing.
