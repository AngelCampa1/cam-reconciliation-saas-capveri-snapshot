Move-outs create small CAM math traps.

The tenant share is not always one annual percentage applied once.

If a tenant moves out mid-year, expands, contracts, or changes suites, the calculation may need date ranges:

Old square footage for one period.
New square footage for the next.
Lease days applied to each period.
Then the adjusted CAM pool.

That is easy to miss when the rent roll is pulled only at year-end.

The source page makes the sequence clear: calculate the share first, then prorate for the tenant's lease days in the reconciliation period.

A move-out reconciliation is cleaner when the packet shows the timing, not just the final true-up.
