An estimate letter should have backup before it has a PDF.

At minimum, the file should answer:

What prior-year actuals were used?
What vendor changes were assumed?
What inflation or rate movement was applied?
What tenant share was used?
What monthly amount was billed?
What changed during the year?

That does not make the estimate perfect.

It makes the estimate explainable when the year-end reconciliation lands.

The worst time to document the estimate logic is after the tenant asks why the true-up moved.
