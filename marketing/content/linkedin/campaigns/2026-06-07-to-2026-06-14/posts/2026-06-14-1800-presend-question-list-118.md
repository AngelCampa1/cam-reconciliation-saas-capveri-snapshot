Carousel outline: Questions to ask before CAM statements go out

Slide 1: The pre-send review is where disputes get cheaper
- Catch the issue before the tenant packet is final.

Slide 2: Denominator
- Does each tenant share match the lease and current measurement support?

Slide 3: Gross-up
- Was the correct occupancy method used, and only on variable expenses?

Slide 4: Exclusions
- Did the material lease exclusions get checked against the pool?

Slide 5: Management fee
- Does the method, base, rate, and cap interaction match the clause?

Slide 6: Capital items
- Were capital items excluded or amortized according to the lease?

Slide 7: Records
- Can the team produce the invoice, worksheet, rent roll, and statement support?

Slide 8: The standard
- Do not send a number the packet cannot defend.
