Gross-up is where a clean-looking CAM statement can go sideways fast.

The formula is not the hard part:

Variable expenses / actual occupancy x target occupancy.

The hard part is deciding what is allowed into "variable expenses."

Common area electric may vary with occupancy.
Janitorial may vary with occupancy.
Trash removal may vary with occupancy.

Property taxes do not.
Insurance premiums do not.
Management fees usually need separate treatment unless the lease ties them to usage-based operating costs.

That one classification pass can change the whole pool before pro-rata share even enters the conversation.

CapVeri keeps that step explicit because the lease math is only as good as the expense labels feeding it.
