There is a clean place for AI in CAM reconciliation.

Reading messy documents.

Lease PDFs, clause extraction, GL classification, and anomaly flags can all benefit from AI assistance. That does not mean the model should decide what a tenant owes.

CapVeri's position is deliberately split:

AI can help extract.
Humans verify the extracted values.
Deterministic logic calculates the dollars.
The ledger stores the path.

That split matters because a tenant dispute is not asking whether the output sounded reasonable.

It is asking whether the number can be traced and reproduced.

The safer architecture is not anti-AI. It is anti-black-box math.
