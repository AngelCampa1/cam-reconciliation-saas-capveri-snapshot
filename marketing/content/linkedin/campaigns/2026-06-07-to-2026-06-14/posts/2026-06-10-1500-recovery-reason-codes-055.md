A recovery gap without reason codes is just a number.

Useful reason codes are specific:

Unmapped GL account.
Lease-excluded category.
Cap limitation.
Gross-up not applied.
Vacancy absorption.
Tenant setup issue.
Estimate billing shortfall.

That list turns a portfolio-level gap into work the team can assign.

Some items may be recoverable.

Some may be permanently excluded.

Some may point to setup controls for next year.

The important part is not pretending the gap is one thing.

It rarely is.
