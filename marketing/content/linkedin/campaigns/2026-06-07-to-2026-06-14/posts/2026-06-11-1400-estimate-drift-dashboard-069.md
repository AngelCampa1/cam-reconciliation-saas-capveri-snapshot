Estimate drift should be visible before the year-end statement.

The useful dashboard is not fancy:

Budget by category.
Actuals to date.
Annualized run rate.
Known contract changes.
Tax and insurance movement.
Estimate billings collected.
Projected true-up.

That view gives the property team a chance to decide whether a mid-year estimate adjustment is worth discussing under the lease.

Without it, too many variances wait until reconciliation season.

The math may be right at year-end.

The surprise still creates the phone call.
