Yardi Charge Schedule belongs in the file when the dispute is about billing history.

Recovery Analysis answers what the reconciliation says.

GL Analytics answers what expenses were in the pool.

Rent Roll with Lease Charges answers who and how much square footage.

Charge Schedule answers what was actually billed through the year.

That matters for:

Mid-year estimate changes.
Charge-code questions.
Invoice-level support.
Proof that the billed side moved when the budget changed.

The mistake is asking one export to answer the whole question.

CAM review works better when each file has a job.
