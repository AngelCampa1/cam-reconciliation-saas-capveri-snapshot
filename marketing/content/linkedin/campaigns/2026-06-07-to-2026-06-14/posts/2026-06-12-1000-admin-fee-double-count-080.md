Management fee review needs one uncomfortable check:

Was the same cost counted twice?

It can happen when the management company invoice sits in the GL as an operating expense and the reconciliation also adds a percentage admin fee on top.

The file should answer:

Is the fee actual or imputed?
Which lease clause allows it?
What base was used?
What rate was applied?
Is there a cap?
Are related GL accounts already in the pool?

That last question is the trap.

The fee line and the GL detail need to agree on what has already been recovered.
