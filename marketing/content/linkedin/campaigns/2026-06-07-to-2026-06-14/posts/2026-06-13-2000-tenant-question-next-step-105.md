When a tenant asks why CAM changed, the weakest answer is:

"That is what the system calculated."

The better next step is to identify which question they are really asking.

Did the denominator change?
Was gross-up applied?
Did an excluded category enter the pool?
Did the management fee base move?
Was a capital item amortized or expensed?
Did the estimate lag actual costs?

Each one needs different support.

The best packets make that triage faster. They do not ask the accounting team to recreate the reconciliation from memory while the property manager waits for a reply.

CapVeri is designed for that moment: source file, lease rule, calculation trail, and exception note in one place.
