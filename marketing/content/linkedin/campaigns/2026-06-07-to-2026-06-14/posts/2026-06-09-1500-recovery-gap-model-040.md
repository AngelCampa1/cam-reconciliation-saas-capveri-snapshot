Treat recovery ratio like a smoke alarm.

When it drops, the answer is not automatically "bill more."

The first move is the comparison:

Recoverable operating expense pool.
Total CAM billed.
Difference by property.
Reason codes for the gap.

The reason codes matter more than the headline number.

A gap caused by a lease exclusion is not the same as a gap caused by an unmapped GL account.

A cap limitation is not the same as a missed gross-up.

The review should make the difference traceable enough for the team to decide what is recoverable, what is excluded, and what needs a setup fix.
