Each material CAM statement line should have a lineage.

Not a paragraph of explanation.

A lineage:

GL account.
Invoice support.
Lease treatment.
Gross-up status.
Cap status.
Tenant share.
Estimate credit.

When that chain is visible, review gets calmer.

When it is missing, a simple tenant question can turn into a reconstruction project.

CapVeri pushes for source-linked statements because CAM confidence is not created by the final PDF. It is created by the records behind it.

The statement is the output.

The lineage is the control.
