Cumulative CAM caps are fragile during handoffs.

The number that matters is not only this year's cap ceiling.

It is the unused capacity carried from prior years, if the lease allows banking.

When a property changes ownership or the accounting seat turns over, that bank can disappear into old spreadsheets.

A useful handoff file should include:

Lease language for cap type.
Base amount.
Annual cap percentage.
Actual controllable expenses by year.
Amount billed by year.
Bank balance by year.
Expiration or renewal notes.

If the lease is non-cumulative, say that clearly too.

Silence is how the wrong cap logic survives into the next reconciliation.
