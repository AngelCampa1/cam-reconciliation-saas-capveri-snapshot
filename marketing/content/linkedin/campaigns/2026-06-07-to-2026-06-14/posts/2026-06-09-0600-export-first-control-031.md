The cleanest CAM review often starts with a file question:

Do the exports tie before anyone opens the workbook?

For Yardi teams, that means the Recovery Analysis, GL Analytics, and rent roll need the same property, the same reconciliation period, and detail-level output.

If one file is summary-level, the math may still look polished. The support will not.

That is where the control lives.

Export first. Tie the files. Then test the lease math.

What is the first export your team checks before statements go out?
