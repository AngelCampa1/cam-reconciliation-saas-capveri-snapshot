Carousel outline: The denominator check that changes CAM

Slide 1: Pro-rata share is not only tenant square footage
- The denominator carries just as much risk.

Slide 2: Pull the lease definition
- Total building area, leasable area, component area, and anchor exclusions are different inputs.

Slide 3: Compare to the rent roll
- The square footage in the system should match the lease-defined method.

Slide 4: Look for vacancy leakage
- Leased area can shrink when suites are vacant. Leasable area usually does not.

Slide 5: Check special pools
- Retail, office, parking, and mixed-use components may need separate denominators.

Slide 6: Save the proof
- Lease clause, rent roll, measurement support, and final percentage.

Slide 7: The control
- A small denominator change can move tenant shares.
