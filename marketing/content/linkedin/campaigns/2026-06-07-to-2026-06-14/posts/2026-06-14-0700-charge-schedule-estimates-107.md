Sometimes the CAM question is not "what did we spend?"

It is "what did we bill?"

That is when the Yardi Charge Schedule becomes useful support.

Recovery Analysis shows the reconciliation result. GL Analytics shows the expense detail. Rent Roll with Lease Charges supports tenant roster and square footage.

Charge Schedule helps when the question turns to estimate billing:

Mid-year estimate changes.
Charge-code disputes.
Invoice-level billing support.
Whether the billed side moved when the budget changed.

It should not replace the core export stack. It should support the billing side when the tenant asks how monthly estimates turned into the final true-up.

Clean reconciliation support separates spend evidence from billing evidence. Mixing those two is how simple questions become long calls.
