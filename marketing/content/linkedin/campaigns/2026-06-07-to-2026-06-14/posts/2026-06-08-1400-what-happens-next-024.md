The useful question after a tenant flags a CAM charge is:

What happens next?

Not "who made the mistake?"
Not "can we explain this away?"

What happens next?

If the GL supports it, produce the invoice and clause.

If the math is wrong, recalculate and document the correction.

If the lease language is ambiguous, separate the interpretation issue from the arithmetic.

If the request is outside scope, say why with the lease language attached.

CapVeri is built for that response path: source record, lease rule, calculation, review status.

That is a better posture than searching through old folders while the clock is already running.
