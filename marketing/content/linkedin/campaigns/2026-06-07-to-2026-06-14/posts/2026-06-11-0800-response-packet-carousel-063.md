Carousel outline: The first CAM documentation response packet

Slide 1: Do not start with every invoice
- Start with the records that explain the reconciliation.

Slide 2: Reconciliation statement
- The exact statement sent to the tenant.

Slide 3: Calculation workbook
- Expense pools, pro-rata shares, gross-up, caps, and estimate credits.

Slide 4: GL detail
- Recoverable accounts for the audit year, with account, vendor, date, and amount.

Slide 5: Rent roll
- Tenant, suite, square footage, lease dates, and share percentage.

Slide 6: Tax and insurance support
- Bills, assessments, declarations, or summary backup tied to the year.

Slide 7: Redaction check
- Remove other tenant terms, payment history, and internal communications.

Slide 8: The control
- Send enough structure for review without turning the first response into a data dump.
