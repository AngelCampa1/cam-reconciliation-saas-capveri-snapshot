There is a simple test for CAM math:

Can the same inputs produce the same answer again?

Not close.
Not plausible.
The same.

Financial math needs deterministic logic. Gross-up, pro-rata share, caps, exclusions, estimates, and settlement amounts need explicit formulas and retrievable inputs.

AI can flag lease clauses and source categories. Humans verify them before calculations use them.

It should not be the calculator.

When a tenant, CPA, asset manager, or property controller asks for the path, the answer should be a ledger, not a paragraph.

Same source file.
Same rule.
Same calculation.
Same result.

That is the bar for a reconciliation packet that has to survive questions later.
