A tenant documentation request starts two clocks.

One clock is contractual.

The lease sets the audit window, response deadline, and lookback period that may apply.

The other clock is operational.

How fast can the team locate the statement, workbook, GL detail, rent roll, invoices, tax support, insurance support, and lease abstract?

The second clock is the one the property team controls.

If the packet is already structured, the response starts with review.

If it is scattered, the response starts with searching.

The response is stronger when the source files and calculation trail are already in one packet.
