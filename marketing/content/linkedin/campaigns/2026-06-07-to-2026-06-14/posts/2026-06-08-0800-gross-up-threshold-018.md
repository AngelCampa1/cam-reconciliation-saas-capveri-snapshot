90% and 95% are not interchangeable gross-up targets.

On $500,000 of variable expenses at 70% occupancy:

Gross-up to 90%:
$500,000 / 0.70 x 0.90 = $642,857

Gross-up to 95%:
$500,000 / 0.70 x 0.95 = $678,571

Difference:
$35,714 in the variable expense pool.

The spreadsheet may treat that as one input cell.

The lease treats it as the rule.

CapVeri keeps the target tied to the lease clause because a clean formula with the wrong threshold is still the wrong calculation.
