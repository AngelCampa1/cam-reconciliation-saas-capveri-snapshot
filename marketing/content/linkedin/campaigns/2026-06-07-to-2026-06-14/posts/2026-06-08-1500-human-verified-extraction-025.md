The safest place for AI in CAM reconciliation is before the math, not inside it.

Use it to extract lease clauses.
Use it to identify likely expense categories.
Use it to surface the fields a human needs to verify.

Then stop.

The calculation should run on confirmed inputs and explicit formulas.

That split matters because extraction can be reviewed. Math has to be reproducible.

CapVeri keeps AI-assisted document work separate from deterministic calculation for exactly that reason.

No model should be asked to "probably" allocate a tenant's charge.

The ledger should show the work.
