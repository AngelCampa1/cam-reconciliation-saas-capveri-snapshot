Before a reconciliation statement leaves the building, the review should answer four plain questions.

Do the exports tie?

Do the lease rules match the calculation inputs?

Can the packet explain the big movements?

Can the team produce support without rebuilding the workbook?

That is the operating standard behind CapVeri.

Export-first, because the source systems already hold the records.

Deterministic math, because financial calculations need to reproduce.

Human verification, because extracted lease terms and classifications still need judgment.

Traceable packets, because tenant questions do not wait for someone to remember how last year's workbook worked.

CAM reconciliation gets stronger when the statement is not just cleaner, but easier to prove.
