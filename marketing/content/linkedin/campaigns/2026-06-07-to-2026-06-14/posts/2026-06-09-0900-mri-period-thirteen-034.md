An MRI export can run clean and still miss the reconciliation answer.

One quiet reason: adjustment periods.

If period 13 or 14 belongs in the close process, a period 12 GL export can look complete while leaving year-end activity outside the file.

That is not a cap problem.

It is not a pro-rata problem.

It is an export boundary problem.

Before testing formulas, check entity, property, period range, and whether adjustment periods are intentionally included or intentionally excluded.

"We used the report" is not the same as "we exported the right accounting period."
