Recovery gap math is useful only when it stays close to the source files.

The basic question:

How much of the recoverable operating expense pool was actually billed back through CAM?

That can point the team toward specific review paths:

New GL accounts not mapped to recovery pools.
Gross-up not applied when the lease permits it.
Recoverable maintenance treated as capital.
Denominator drift.
Cap rules suppressing recovery.

But a low ratio does not automatically mean the landlord can bill more. The lease still controls, and the source support still has to hold up.

The recovery-gap view works best as a triage tool:

Find the property.
Find the category.
Then test the actual rule.
