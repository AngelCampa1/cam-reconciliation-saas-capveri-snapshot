A defensible CAM packet is not a folder full of everything.

It is the right records in the right order.

For a tenant documentation request, the standard first layer usually looks like:

GL detail for recoverable accounts
Reconciliation worksheets
Rent roll
Statement sent to the tenant
Tax bills and assessment notices
Insurance support

Then come targeted invoices, service contracts, management fee support, and utility detail when the lease or the request calls for them.

The mistake is either under-producing or dumping a messy data room on the auditor.

Neither builds confidence.

CapVeri's bias is toward a traceable packet: the number, the rule, the source, and the support that lets someone rerun the logic.
