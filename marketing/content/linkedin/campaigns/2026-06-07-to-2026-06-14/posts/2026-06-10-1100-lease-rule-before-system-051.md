"The system calculated it" is not enough support for CAM.

The system still needs instructions.

Gross-up threshold.
Expense exclusions.
Cap type.
Denominator.
Admin fee method.
Partial-year treatment.

Those are lease rules before they are software settings.

CapVeri's export-first approach keeps the ERP as the source system and runs a traceable calculation layer beside it.

That matters because a reconciliation question rarely starts with "which button was clicked?"

It starts with "why did this tenant get this number?"

The answer needs a source file, a lease rule, and math that can be rerun.
