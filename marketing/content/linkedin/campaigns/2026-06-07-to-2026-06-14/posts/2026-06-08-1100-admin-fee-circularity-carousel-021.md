Carousel outline: The admin fee circularity problem

Slide 1: "15% of CAM" may not be enough
- The base decides the fee.

Slide 2: Net method
- Fee = rate x expense base.

Slide 3: Gross method
- Fee is included in the total that the fee is based on.

Slide 4: The circularity
- The fee depends on a number that includes the fee.

Slide 5: The algebraic fix
- Fee = rate x base / (1 - rate).

Slide 6: The capped method
- Calculate the fee, then apply the lease ceiling.

Slide 7: The audit packet
- Keep the lease clause and worksheet together.

Slide 8: The control
- Method first. Percentage second.
