The estimate letter is where expectations get set.

The reconciliation is where the actuals catch up.

If the estimate was stale all year, the true-up may be mathematically correct and still feel like a surprise.

That is why a mid-year check matters:

Current GL run rate.
Budget assumptions.
Vendor changes.
Insurance and tax movement.
Tenant estimate billings to date.

The goal is not to make the estimate perfect.

It is to catch obvious drift before it becomes the only thing the tenant remembers about the reconciliation.
