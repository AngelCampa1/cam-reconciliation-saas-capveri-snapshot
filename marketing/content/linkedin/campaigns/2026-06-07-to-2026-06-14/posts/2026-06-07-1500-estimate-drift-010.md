Estimate drift is how a calm monthly CAM charge becomes an ugly year-end conversation.

The monthly estimate was set in advance.
The GL kept moving.
Vendor contracts changed.
Utility costs changed.
Insurance renewals changed.

Then the reconciliation lands as one lump-sum true-up.

The math can be correct and still feel like a surprise because the run rate was not checked mid-year.

CapVeri models the gap between estimated billings and adjusted actuals so teams can see the issue before the statement is sent.

This is not about promising a lower bill.

It is about knowing whether the true-up is explainable before a tenant asks.
