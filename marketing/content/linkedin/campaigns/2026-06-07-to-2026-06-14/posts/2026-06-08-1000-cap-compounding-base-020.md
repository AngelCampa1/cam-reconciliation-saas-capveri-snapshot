One CAM cap error stays quiet until expenses spike:

Compounding from last year's actual bill instead of last year's cap ceiling.

If actuals were below the ceiling, the two bases separate.

Year after year, the incorrect ceiling drifts lower. In a calm expense year, the issue may stay hidden because actuals are still under both numbers.

Then insurance, security, or maintenance jumps.

Suddenly the cap binds, and the building is using a ceiling that was quietly underbuilt for years.

CapVeri tracks the cap path from the prior ceiling, not from whatever happened to be billed last year.

That distinction is dull until it matters.

Then it is the whole calculation.
