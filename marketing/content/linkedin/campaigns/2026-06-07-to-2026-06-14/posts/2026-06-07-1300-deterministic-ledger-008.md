AI can help read messy lease documents.

It should not be the thing deciding CAM dollars.

The calculation needs to be reproducible:

Same inputs.
Same lease rules.
Same result.

That matters when someone asks where a charge came from 18 months later.

CapVeri uses AI-assisted extraction for documents, with human verification before extracted values enter the calculation flow. The math itself is deterministic and traceable.

Gross-up inputs, pro-rata denominator, cap path, estimates billed, final true-up, each step belongs in a ledger.

The answer should not be "the model said so."

It should be a number that can be rebuilt.
