MRI Tenant Billing History is not always the first file to pull.

But when the question is "what did we bill during the year?" it matters.

Recovery Reconciliation can show the recovery result.

GL Transaction Detail can show what hit the expense pool.

The rent roll can show who was in the building and what square footage applied.

Tenant Billing History answers a different question:

Which estimates were actually invoiced, and did a mid-year estimate change reach the tenant?

That is the difference between checking spend and checking collection.

Both sides of the reconciliation need support.
