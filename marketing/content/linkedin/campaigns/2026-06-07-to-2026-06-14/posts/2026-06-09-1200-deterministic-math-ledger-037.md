AI can help read documents.

CAM math needs a ledger.

The calculation has to answer ordinary questions:

Which lease clause controlled the pool?
Which expenses were included or excluded?
Which gross-up threshold was used?
Which denominator produced the tenant share?
Which cap ceiling limited the amount?

That is why CapVeri keeps extraction and calculation separate.

Extraction can be reviewed by a human.

The math runs through deterministic rules so the same inputs produce the same answer.

For financial work, "show the work" is not a nice-to-have.

It is the point.
