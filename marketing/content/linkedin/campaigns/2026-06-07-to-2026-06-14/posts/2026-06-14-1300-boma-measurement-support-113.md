If a portfolio adopts BOMA 2024, the measurement support has to travel with the CAM math.

The system update is only one step.

The packet should still show:

Prior measurement.
New measurement.
Tenant rentable square footage.
Building total rentable area.
Pro-rata share before and after.
Effective date.
Lease language that permits the change, or the lease event where it applies.

That sounds like extra paperwork until the first tenant asks why their share changed.

CapVeri can support calculation workflows that use BOMA-referenced measurement inputs, but the source documentation remains the control. The measurement standard, lease permission, and denominator update all need to be visible.

No certification claim needed. Just a cleaner trail.
