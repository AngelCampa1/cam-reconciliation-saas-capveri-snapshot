The danger with AI in CAM work is not that it reads documents.

That part can help.

The danger is letting a plausible extraction become a financial answer without a verification step.

A gross-up clause still needs the occupancy threshold.
An exclusion still needs the expense category.
A cap clause still needs the base, year, and carry-forward rule.

Those are inputs.

Once the inputs are confirmed, the calculation should be boringly repeatable:

Same source file.
Same lease rule.
Same formula.
Same answer.

Assistance is useful.

Authority belongs to the source trail.
