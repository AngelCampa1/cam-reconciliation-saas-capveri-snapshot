Carousel outline: Partial-year pro-rata review

Slide 1: Annual share is not enough
- Move-ins, move-outs, expansions, and contractions need date ranges.

Slide 2: Start with tenant RSF
- Use the rentable area from the lease or controlling source.

Slide 3: Confirm the denominator
- Use the denominator defined by that tenant's lease.

Slide 4: Calculate the share
- Tenant RSF divided by the lease-defined denominator.

Slide 5: Split the year
- Build one row for each effective date range.

Slide 6: Apply the pool
- Use the adjusted expense pool that applies to the period.

Slide 7: Credit estimates
- Subtract what was billed or collected during that period.

Slide 8: Leave the schedule
- The timing support is the control, not a footnote.
