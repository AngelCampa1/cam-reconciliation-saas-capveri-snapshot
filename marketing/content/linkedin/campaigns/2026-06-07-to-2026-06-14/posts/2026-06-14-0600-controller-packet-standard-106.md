A strong CAM-season controller is not the one with the most complicated workbook.

It is the one who can answer:

Where did this number come from?
Which lease rule drove it?
Which inputs changed?
Can the same inputs produce the same answer again?

That is why deterministic math matters.

For extraction, AI can help find lease clauses and classify messy source material. But before dollars move, those extracted values need human review.

For the calculation itself, reproducibility is the standard.

Same inputs.
Same formulas.
Same result.
Retrievable later.

That is not a branding line. It is the difference between a packet that explains itself and a packet that depends on whoever built the workbook last year.
