The first 30 days after a tenant audit notice are mostly about control.

Who owns the response?
Which lease clause governs the request?
Which years are in scope?
Which records can be produced without scrambling?
Which disputed items are math errors, ambiguous lease reads, or unsupported claims?

That sorting work matters because a blanket denial is rarely useful.

A stronger response says:

This item is conceded.
This item is supported by these records.
This item depends on this lease interpretation.
This item is outside the documented scope.

CapVeri helps build that trail before the notice arrives, so the response starts from files and calculations instead of memory.
