Move-outs make annual pro-rata math easy to get wrong.

A tenant that leaves on June 30 should not be treated like a full-year tenant.

A tenant that expands on July 1 should not have one annual numerator.

The year needs to be split into the periods the lease actually occupied:

Before move-out.
After move-out.
Before expansion.
After expansion.

Then the share gets calculated for each period and prorated by lease days.

CapVeri handles that as date-bound math, not a year-end shortcut.

Because the rent roll snapshot is a starting point, not the whole story.
