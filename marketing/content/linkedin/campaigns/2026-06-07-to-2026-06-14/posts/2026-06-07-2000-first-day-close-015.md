The last hour before CAM statements go out is the wrong time to discover the support file is incomplete.

The pre-send check should answer plain questions:

Do tenant shares sum correctly?
Did fixed costs stay out of gross-up?
Did caps apply to the right expense pool?
Do invoices support the GL?
Does the delivery window still work for each lease?

None of that sounds strategic.

It is still the difference between sending a statement and sending a statement the team can stand behind.

CapVeri is built around that pre-send moment: find the fragile math before a tenant, auditor, or asset manager has to point at it.

What is the check your team refuses to skip?
