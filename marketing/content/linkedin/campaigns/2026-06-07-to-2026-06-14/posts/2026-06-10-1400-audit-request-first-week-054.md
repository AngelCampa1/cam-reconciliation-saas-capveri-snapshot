The first week after a CAM audit request should not be spent asking where the documents are.

It should be spent checking that the packet matches the reconciliation that was sent.

That means:

Final GL export.
Invoice index.
Management fee backup.
Pro-rata schedule.
Gross-up workbook.
Cap schedule.
Tax and insurance support.
Lease amendment register.

The packet does not need drama.

It needs version control.

If the team has to recreate the support on demand, each manual step creates another place for the story to change.
