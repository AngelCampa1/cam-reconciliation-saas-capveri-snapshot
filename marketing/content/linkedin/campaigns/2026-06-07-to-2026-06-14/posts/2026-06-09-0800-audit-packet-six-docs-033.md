Carousel outline: What belongs in the CAM audit packet?

Slide 1: The packet is the response
- A tenant audit request should not start a document hunt.

Slide 2: GL export
- Full year, property level, with account codes and descriptions.

Slide 3: Invoice index
- Vendor, date, amount, account, and a way to pull the backup fast.

Slide 4: Management fee calculation
- Base, rate, cap if any, and the lease clause used.

Slide 5: Pro-rata schedule
- Tenant RSF, denominator, percentage, and source for each number.

Slide 6: Gross-up workbook
- Occupancy, threshold, fixed-variable split, and normalized variable pool.

Slide 7: Cap bank schedule
- Base year, annual ceiling, actuals, unused capacity, and carry-forward balance.

Slide 8: The point
- Make the review traceable before the question arrives.
