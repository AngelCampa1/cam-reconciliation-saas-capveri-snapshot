Move-out reconciliations break when the year is treated as one flat period.

A tenant that leaves in May does not need the same date range as a tenant that stayed through December.

The control is simple:

Calculate the share.
Apply the correct expense pool.
Prorate by the lease days in the reconciliation period.
Tie the estimate billings already collected.

The mistake is skipping straight to the annual tenant percentage and hoping the timing works itself out.

It usually does not.

Partial-year occupancy needs its own row, not a footnote.
