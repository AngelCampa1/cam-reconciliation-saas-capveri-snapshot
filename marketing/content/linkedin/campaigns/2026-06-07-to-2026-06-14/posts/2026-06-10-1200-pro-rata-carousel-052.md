Carousel outline: The pro-rata share check

Slide 1: The percentage is not the whole story
- A clean-looking share can still use the wrong denominator.

Slide 2: Confirm the numerator
- Tenant rentable SF from the lease abstract or controlling measurement source.

Slide 3: Confirm the denominator
- Building leasable area, or the lease-defined denominator.

Slide 4: Watch leased area
- Vacant space should not disappear unless the lease says the method allows it.

Slide 5: Watch excluded anchors
- If anchors are excluded, the effective denominator changes for inline tenants.

Slide 6: Watch remeasurement
- BOMA updates can move RSF and pro-rata shares when the lease permits it.

Slide 7: Watch partial-year changes
- Expansions, contractions, move-ins, and move-outs need date ranges.

Slide 8: Keep the schedule
- The tenant percentage needs a source, not just a cell value.
