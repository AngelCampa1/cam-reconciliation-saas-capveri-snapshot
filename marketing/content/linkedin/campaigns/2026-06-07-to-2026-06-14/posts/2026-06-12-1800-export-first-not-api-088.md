For CAM reconciliation, the export is often the right boundary.

The ERP remains the system of record.

The review layer needs clean files:

Rent roll.
GL detail.
Recovery output.
Billing history when needed.

That is different from promising a deep integration or replacing the property management system.

CapVeri's position is deliberately narrower:

Get the source files out.
Validate the fields.
Map the lease rules.
Run deterministic math.
Keep the support packet.

That is enough work without pretending the ERP should disappear.
