A useful CAM calculation should let a reviewer rerun the answer from the inputs.

That reviewer might be internal accounting.

It might be a CPA.

It might be the person who inherits the file two years from now.

Either way, the file needs more than a final number.

It needs:

The source export.
The lease rule.
The classification decision.
The formula.
The exact inputs.
The final tenant result.

CapVeri's math is built around that trace.

AI can help extract lease and document facts, but verified inputs and deterministic calculations are what make the result repeatable.
