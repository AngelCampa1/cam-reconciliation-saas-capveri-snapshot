Gross-up has a stop sign.

If the lease target is 95% and the building is already at or above 95%, the gross-up factor should not inflate the variable pool.

That sounds obvious.

It still needs to be checked, because a spreadsheet can apply the formula mechanically:

Target occupancy / actual occupancy.

Without the threshold guard, the calculation can move when it should stay at actuals.

CapVeri treats gross-up as a rule set, not just a formula:

Only variable expenses.
Only when the lease allows it.
Only to the lease-defined target.
No inflation above the target condition.

Small guardrails help catch expensive-looking mistakes before they harden into the packet.
