Carousel outline: The gross-up review starts before the formula

Slide 1: First split the pool
- Gross-up is a classification problem before it is a math problem.

Slide 2: Ask what occupancy changes
- Some costs move with occupancy. Others stay close to fixed.

Slide 3: Keep fixed costs out
- Taxes, insurance, and many management fees need separate treatment unless the lease says otherwise.

Slide 4: Then apply the target
- The lease-defined target belongs on eligible variable costs only.

Slide 5: Add back after review
- Fixed costs may still be recoverable. They are just not inflated by occupancy.

Slide 6: The support file needs labels
- Variable, fixed, excluded, capped, and disputed should not be hidden in formulas.

Slide 7: The audit question
- Which costs changed because occupancy changed, and where is that shown?
