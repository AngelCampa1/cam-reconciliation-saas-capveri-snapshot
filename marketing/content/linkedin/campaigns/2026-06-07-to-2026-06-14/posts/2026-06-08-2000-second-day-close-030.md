The reconciliation question is not only:

What did the property spend?

It is also:

What did the tenant already pay?

A strong true-up needs both sides:

Adjusted expense pool.
Lease-defined pro-rata share.
Any allowed gross-up or cap logic.
Estimated billings collected during the year.
Final balance due or credit.

Miss the estimate credit, and the statement can be wrong even if the expense math is right.

CapVeri ties billing history back into the calculation because reconciliation is not a GL report.

It is a settlement.
