Carousel outline: Gross-up review in the right order

Slide 1: Gross-up is not one number
- It is a lease rule plus an expense classification.

Slide 2: Start with the clause
- Does the lease permit gross-up, and what target does it define?

Slide 3: Confirm actual occupancy
- Use the period and measurement basis the lease requires.

Slide 4: Split the pool
- Fixed costs stay fixed. Variable costs are the gross-up candidates.

Slide 5: Apply the target
- Variable expenses divided by actual occupancy, multiplied by target occupancy.

Slide 6: Add fixed costs back
- Do not gross up the whole operating pool.

Slide 7: Keep the workbook
- Occupancy, target, classifications, and final pool need to be retrievable.

Slide 8: The control
- The formula is simple. The evidence around it is the work.
