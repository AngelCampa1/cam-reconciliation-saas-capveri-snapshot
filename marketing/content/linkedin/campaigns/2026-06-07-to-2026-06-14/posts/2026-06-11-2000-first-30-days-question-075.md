Carousel outline: First 30 days after a CAM documentation request

Slide 1: Day 1
- Pull the lease and confirm the audit clause.

Slide 2: Days 1 to 5
- Re-run the math internally before producing records.

Slide 3: Exposure check
- Identify the largest possible issue before the tenant does.

Slide 4: Records package
- Statement, workbook, GL detail, rent roll, tax, insurance, and requested support.

Slide 5: Redaction
- Remove other tenant terms and internal communications.

Slide 6: Delivery
- Use a controlled file share and keep one contact point.

Slide 7: Follow-up
- Track questions by document, not by email thread memory.

Slide 8: The question
- Does the team have this workflow before the letter arrives?
