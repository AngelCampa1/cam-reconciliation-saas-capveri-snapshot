For MRI CAM review, three files carry much of the load:

Recovery Reconciliation.
GL Transaction Detail.
Rent roll.

The first shows the tenant recovery result.

The second proves what fed the expense pool.

The third gives the tenant roster, rentable SF, and timing.

If tenant IDs or suites are missing, matching turns fragile fast.

If the GL is summary-level, classification gets harder than it needs to be.

If the entity is wrong, the rest of the review is noise.

Export QA is not admin work. It is the control that decides whether the math review starts on solid ground.
