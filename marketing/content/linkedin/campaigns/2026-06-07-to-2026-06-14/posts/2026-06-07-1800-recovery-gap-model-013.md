A recovery gap is a model, not a promise.

That distinction matters.

If a property has recoverable operating expenses that are not reaching tenant billings, the next step is not to declare "found money."

The next step is to explain the gap:

Was a new GL account left out of a recovery pool?
Was gross-up missed below the lease threshold?
Was a cap suppressing valid recovery?
Was an expense excluded by the lease?

CapVeri models the gap so teams can triage it before making a billing decision.

Some gaps are recoverable.
Some are lease-constrained.
Some are classification noise.

The point is to know which is which.
