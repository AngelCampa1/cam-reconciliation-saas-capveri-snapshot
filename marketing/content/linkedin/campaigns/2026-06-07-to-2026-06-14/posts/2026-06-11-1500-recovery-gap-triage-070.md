Recovery gap analysis should separate modeling from action.

Modeling says:

Recoverable operating expense pool.
Total CAM billed.
Gap by property.
Gap by category.
Likely reason code.

Action says:

Can this be corrected under the lease?
Is the billing window still open?
Is the issue a current-year setup fix instead?
Does the file need owner, accountant, or counsel review?

CapVeri can help identify the gap and the likely source.

That is not the same as saying the full gap is collectible.

The cleanest recovery work keeps that distinction visible.
