MRI export QA starts with three labels that sound small until one is wrong:

Entity.
Property.
Period.

If any one is wrong, the reconciliation can still look polished.

It just will not tie.

The entity may hold the operating expense.
The property may drive the recovery setup.
The period may need adjustment months included or excluded intentionally.

Those decisions come before the math review.

A clean export packet should make them visible on the core files:

Recovery Reconciliation.
GL Transaction Detail.
Rent roll.
Tenant Billing History when billing support is needed.
