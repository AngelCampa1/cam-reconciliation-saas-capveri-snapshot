A cap can be applied to the right expenses and still drift.

One common source is the compounding base.

If the lease says the cap grows from the prior cap ceiling, but the workbook grows it from the prior billed amount, the ceiling can fall behind year after year.

That error may stay hidden while actuals are below the cap.

It shows up when expenses spike and the ceiling finally matters.

The check:

Find the base year.
Find the allowed annual increase.
Find the prior ceiling.
Recalculate the current ceiling from the lease rule.

Do that before debating the variance.
