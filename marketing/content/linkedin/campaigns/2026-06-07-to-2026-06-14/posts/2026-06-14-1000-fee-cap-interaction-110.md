A management fee clause and a CAM cap clause can collide.

The reconciliation has to answer one question clearly:

Is the fee inside the cap or outside it?

If the fee is inside the cap, it may be limited with other controllable expenses.

If the fee is outside the cap, it may flow through based on the fee method and base the lease allows.

Then there is a second question:

Is the fee calculated on actual expenses, capped expenses, or a defined subset of the pool?

This is where a one-line "15% admin fee" entry creates more questions than it answers.

The packet should show the clause, the base, the method, and the cap interaction. Anything less asks the reviewer to infer the rule.
