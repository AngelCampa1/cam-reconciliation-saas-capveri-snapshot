The denominator proof file should be plain.

That is the point.

For pro-rata share, the team should be able to produce:

The lease clause.
The tenant rentable square footage.
The building or component denominator.
Any anchor or excluded-area treatment.
The rent roll or measurement support.
The final percentage used in the reconciliation.

If the denominator came from leased area instead of leasable area, that needs to be intentional and lease-supported. If it came from a component pool, the component boundary needs to be visible.

Small denominator differences travel through tenant charges.

The final percentage is not the proof. It is the last line of the proof.
