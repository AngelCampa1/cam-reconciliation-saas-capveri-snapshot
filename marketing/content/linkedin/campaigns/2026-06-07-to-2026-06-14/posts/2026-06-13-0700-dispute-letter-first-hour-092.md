The first hour after a tenant documentation request is not the time to debate the disputed charge.

It is the time to pull the lease.

Before records go out, the team needs to know:

Does the tenant have audit rights?
What years are open?
What response window applies?
Does the auditor need to meet a qualification rule?
What records does the clause actually require?

That sounds procedural. It is also how the accounting team reduces the risk of producing the wrong package under pressure.

The useful response is calm and specific:

Acknowledge the request.
Confirm the lease provision.
Assign one contact.
Prepare the records package.
Track what was produced.

A tenant question gets harder when multiple departments answer different versions of it.
