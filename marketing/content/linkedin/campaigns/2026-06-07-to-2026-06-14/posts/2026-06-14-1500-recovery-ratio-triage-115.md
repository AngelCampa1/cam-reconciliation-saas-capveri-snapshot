A recovery ratio is not a verdict.

It is a triage signal.

Total CAM billed divided by total recoverable operating expenses gives the team a property-level view of whether recovery is behaving as expected.

If the ratio drops, the next step is not "bill more."

The next step is to find the cause:

Was a new GL account left out of the recovery pool?
Was gross-up skipped or misapplied?
Did a denominator change move shares?
Did a cap suppress recovery?
Was a maintenance item treated as capital?

Each answer needs lease support and source support.

The metric points to where the review should start. It does not replace the review.
