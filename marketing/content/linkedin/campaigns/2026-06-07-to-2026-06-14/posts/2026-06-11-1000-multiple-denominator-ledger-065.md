One building can have more than one CAM denominator.

That is where copy-paste math gets dangerous.

One lease may reference total rentable area.
Another may reference gross leasable area.
Another may have an occupied-area floor.
Another may freeze a measurement standard from an older lease form.

The control is a denominator ledger by tenant:

Lease clause.
Interpreted denominator.
Square footage source.
Current value.
Prior-year value.
Reason for any change.

Then each tenant share is calculated from its own rule.

The portfolio view comes after that, with a total recovery check to make sure the allocations do not exceed the recoverable pool.
