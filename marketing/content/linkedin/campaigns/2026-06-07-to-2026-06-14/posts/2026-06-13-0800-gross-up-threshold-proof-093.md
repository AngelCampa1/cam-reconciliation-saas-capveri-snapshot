The difference between a 90% and 95% gross-up target is not wording.

It is a billing input.

The lease has to answer three questions before the calculation is useful:

What occupancy threshold applies?
What occupancy measurement applies?
Which expenses are variable enough to gross up?

The math after that is mechanical:

Variable expenses divided by actual occupancy, multiplied by the lease-defined target.

Fixed expenses come back in unchanged.

The mistake is treating the target as a portfolio habit. One building uses 95% because the lease says 95%. Another uses 90% because the lease says 90%. A third may have no gross-up right at all.

CapVeri keeps that distinction visible in the calculation trail, because a gross-up factor without the clause behind it is just a number.
