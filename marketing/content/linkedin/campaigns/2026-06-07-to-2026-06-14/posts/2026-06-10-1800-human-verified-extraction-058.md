AI is useful when the file is messy.

Lease PDFs are messy.
GL descriptions are messy.
Amendments are messy.
Expense categories are messy.

That is a reading problem.

The charge calculation is different.

Once the clause, category, date, and amount are extracted, the next question is whether a reviewer can confirm the inputs and rerun the formula.

AI can help with the first read.

It should not be the final authority on what a tenant owes.
