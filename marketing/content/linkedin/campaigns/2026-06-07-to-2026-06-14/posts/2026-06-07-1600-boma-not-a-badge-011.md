BOMA 2024 is not a badge to paste onto a CAM statement.

It is a measurement input that can change rentable area, load factor, pro-rata share, and gross-up denominator.

That is why the useful question is not:

"Are we BOMA aligned?"

The useful question is:

"Which measurement standard does each lease allow, and which square footage did the reconciliation use?"

CapVeri's BOMA 2024 work is about applying the measurement inputs consistently. It is not a certification claim. It is not an endorsement claim.

The file needs plain evidence:

Lease language, measurement report, system update, parallel-run check.

That is where the billing risk sits.
