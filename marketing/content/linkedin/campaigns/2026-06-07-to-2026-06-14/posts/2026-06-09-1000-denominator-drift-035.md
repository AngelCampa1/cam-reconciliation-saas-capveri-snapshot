Pro-rata share is a tiny percentage with a large blast radius.

The numerator gets attention because it is the tenant's space.

The denominator is where quiet drift shows up.

Leasable area and leased area are not interchangeable. If vacant suites fall out of the denominator, the occupied tenants carry a larger share of the same pool.

The expense did not change.

The allocation did.

Before sending a reconciliation, compare the denominator to the lease definition and the rent roll source behind it.

That one check can explain a lot of "why did my share move?" questions.
