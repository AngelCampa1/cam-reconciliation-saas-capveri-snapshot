The first reconciliation after a measurement update needs a parallel proof.

Old measurement input.
New measurement input.
Lease language.
Tenant share under each basis.
Expense pool under the current methodology.
Dollar impact.
Effective date.

That comparison is not about turning BOMA 2024 into a badge.

It is about showing which input changed and why the allocation changed.

If the lease references a specific older standard, the file needs to say that too.

If the lease allows an updated standard, the file needs the support.

The math should not ask the tenant to guess which measurement basis was used.
