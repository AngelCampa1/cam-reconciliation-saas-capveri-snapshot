A cumulative CAM cap needs a bank balance.

Not a note in last year's workbook.
Not a memory from the prior property manager.
A bank balance.

The mechanic is straightforward:

If actual controllable expenses are below the cap ceiling, unused room is banked.

If a later year spikes above the standard ceiling, the bank can cover the excess if the lease allows it.

Under a non-cumulative cap, unused room disappears. Under a cumulative cap, it carries forward until drawn or expired by the lease.

That difference can decide whether a spike year is recoverable or absorbed.

CapVeri tracks the cap path because "5% annual cap" is not enough information by itself.
