Carousel outline: Cumulative cap bank review

Slide 1: A cap bank is not a note
- It is a year-by-year schedule.

Slide 2: Confirm the lease type
- Cumulative and non-cumulative caps behave differently.

Slide 3: Start with the base
- Base year controllable amount and tenant share.

Slide 4: Build the ceiling
- Apply the annual cap percentage to the correct prior ceiling.

Slide 5: Compare actuals
- Actual controllable amount versus allowed ceiling.

Slide 6: Track unused capacity
- If cumulative, bank unused capacity for later years.

Slide 7: Track draws
- When actuals exceed the base ceiling, show the bank draw.

Slide 8: Keep it portable
- The schedule should survive a sale, staff change, or audit request.
