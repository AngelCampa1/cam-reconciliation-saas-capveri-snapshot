A spreadsheet can look like data and still behave like a report.

Yardi exports often carry report furniture:

Title rows.
Header bands.
Subtotal rows.
Page totals.
Footer notes.

Those are readable for humans and painful for validation.

Before the CAM file goes into any review process, flatten it.

One row per record.
Column names in one header row.
No hidden totals mixed into transaction data.

This is not cosmetic.

It decides whether the file can be tied, filtered, and traced without accidental double-counting.
