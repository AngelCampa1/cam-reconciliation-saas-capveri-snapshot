Carousel outline: Recovery gap model, not a collection promise

Slide 1: Start with the ratio
- CAM billed divided by recoverable operating expenses.

Slide 2: Build the recoverable pool
- Total OpEx less CapEx, lease exclusions, and landlord-only costs.

Slide 3: Compare billings
- Estimates collected plus year-end true-up amounts.

Slide 4: Split the gap
- GL mapping, gross-up, cap limits, denominator drift, vacancy absorption, or estimate shortfall.

Slide 5: Check the lease
- A modeled gap is not automatically billable.

Slide 6: Check timing
- Billing windows and statement deadlines matter.

Slide 7: Assign the next step
- Fix setup, document exclusion, or escalate the question.

Slide 8: Keep the caveat
- The model points to work. It does not guarantee recovery.
