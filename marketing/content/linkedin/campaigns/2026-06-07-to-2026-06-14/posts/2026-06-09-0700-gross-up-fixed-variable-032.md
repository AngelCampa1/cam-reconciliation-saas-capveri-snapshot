Gross-up mistakes usually start before the formula.

The question is not just "what is the occupancy target?"

It is:

Which expenses are actually variable?

Property taxes do not become 95% occupied. Insurance does not become 95% occupied. A fixed management fee does not become 95% occupied.

Janitorial, utilities, trash, and similar usage-driven costs are the items that need the closer look.

The formula is only defensible after the pool is split.

If the split is wrong, the percentage will not rescue it.
