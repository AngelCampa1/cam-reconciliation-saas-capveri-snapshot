MRI teams have a CAM export trap that does not look like a trap.

The report runs. The property looks right. The recovery output has totals.

But the GL detail may be missing period 13 or 14 adjustments.

That means the reconciliation can look complete while year-end activity is sitting outside the file being reviewed.

The first question is not "why is MRI wrong?"

It is:

Which entity holds the operating expenses, and which adjustment periods belong in the reconciliation year?

Export QA is not clerical work. It is the control that decides whether the math review starts from the full expense pool.
