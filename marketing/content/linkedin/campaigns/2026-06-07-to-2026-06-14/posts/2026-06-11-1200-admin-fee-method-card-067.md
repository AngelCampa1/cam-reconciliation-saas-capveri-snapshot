Carousel outline: Where admin fee checks usually break

Slide 1: Start with the base
- The percentage matters less if the fee is applied to the wrong number.

Slide 2: Failure point 1
- The fee is calculated from the gross pool when the lease points to a net base.

Slide 3: Failure point 2
- The fee is added twice: once as a GL line, once as a percentage.

Slide 4: Failure point 3
- The cap is missed, or applied to the wrong expense group.

Slide 5: The clause language matters
- "Included in" and "added to" can point to different treatments.

Slide 6: The support file should show
- Base, rate, cap if any, GL treatment, and clause reference.

Slide 7: The reviewer question
- Can the fee be recalculated without guessing what base was used?

Slide 8: The practical takeaway
- Debate the percentage only after the base and cap treatment are clean.
