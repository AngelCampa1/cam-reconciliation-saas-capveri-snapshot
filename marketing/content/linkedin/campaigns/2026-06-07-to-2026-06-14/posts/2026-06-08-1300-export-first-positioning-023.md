CapVeri can find CAM risk without becoming the accounting system.

The useful data is already there:

Recovery report.
GL detail.
Rent roll.
Billing history when needed.

The question is whether those exports are complete, flat, tied to the same period, and matched back to lease rules.

That is the export-first view of CAM reconciliation.

No direct ERP integration promise.
No "rip out the system" story.
No black-box math.

Just the files property teams already know, tested against deterministic lease calculations.

Sometimes the fastest path to control is not a deeper integration.

It is a cleaner export.
