An estimate letter should be able to answer one question fast:

What changed?

The useful support is usually short:

New monthly estimate.
Effective date.
Prior amount.
Top expense drivers.
Known contract changes.
Tax or insurance movement.
Lease section for estimate adjustments.
Contact point for questions.

The estimate letter usually does not need a full GL dump.

But if the increase is material, the team should have the driver summary ready before the letter goes out.

That is the difference between sending a number and sending a number with support.
