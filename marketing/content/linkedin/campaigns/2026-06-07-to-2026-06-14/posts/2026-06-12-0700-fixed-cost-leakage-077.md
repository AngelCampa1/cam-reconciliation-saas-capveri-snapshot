Gross-up errors cut both ways.

Grossing up fixed costs can overstate the pool.

Failing to gross up eligible variable costs can leave recoverable expense out of the tenant calculation when the lease permits the adjustment.

The control is the same in both directions:

Classify first.
Check the clause.
Check actual occupancy.
Apply the target only to the eligible variable pool.
Add fixed costs back unchanged.

That sequence is basic, but easy to skip under deadline pressure.

It is also the part that reduces the risk of the same spreadsheet being wrong in opposite directions on different properties.
