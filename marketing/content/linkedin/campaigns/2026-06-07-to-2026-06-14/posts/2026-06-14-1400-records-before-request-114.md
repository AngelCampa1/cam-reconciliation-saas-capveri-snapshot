The best time to build the CAM audit file is before anyone asks for it.

The defense playbook is not mysterious:

Vendor invoices.
Recurring service contracts.
GL detail by property and expense account.
Occupancy schedules.
Management fee worksheets.
Insurance support.
Tax bills and assessment notices.
Capital schedules when needed.

What matters is whether those records connect to the reconciliation statement without a scavenger hunt.

A documentation request is stressful when the file has to be assembled from inboxes, old folders, and someone's memory.

It is calmer when the packet already exists by property, year, category, tenant, and calculation.

That is the operating standard CapVeri is built around: make the support retrievable before the question arrives.
