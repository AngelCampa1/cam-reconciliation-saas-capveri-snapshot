When a tenant's pro-rata share changes, the file needs a short answer ready.

Not a paragraph of theory.

A trace.

Prior denominator.
Current denominator.
Reason for change.
Measurement source.
Lease clause.
Effective date.
Tenant numerator.
Share before and after.

That is the note that keeps a denominator change from looking arbitrary.

If the source is a remeasurement, show it.

If the source is a lease amendment, show it.

If the source is an occupancy provision, show the lease language.

The percentage is the output. The denominator note is the support.
