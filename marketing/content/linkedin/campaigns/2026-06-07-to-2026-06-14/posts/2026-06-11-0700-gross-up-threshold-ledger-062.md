Gross-up support should show more than the final adjusted pool.

It should show the threshold ledger.

Actual occupancy.
Lease target.
Variable expense pool.
Fixed expense pool.
Gross-up factor.
Adjusted variable expense.
Fixed costs added back unchanged.

That order matters.

If the file cannot show which costs stayed fixed, the tenant or reviewer has to infer the treatment from the final number.

That is a weak place to stand.

The calculation is not hard. The hard part is leaving enough evidence that the team can prove the right costs entered the formula.
