A CAM export package needs a column contract.

Not a legal contract.

A practical one.

Rent roll needs tenant, suite, rentable SF, lease start, and lease end.

GL detail needs account, date, vendor or description, and amount.

Recovery output needs tenant, pool, actuals, estimates, and variance.

When those fields are missing, the review turns into matching by memory.

That is where errors creep in.

Before the math starts, CapVeri treats the export package as the first control surface:

Do the files contain the fields needed to trace the statement?
