Two admin fee calculations can use the same percentage and produce different answers.

That is why the first question is method, not rate.

Gross method: the fee is calculated on a pool that includes the fee.

Net method: the fee is calculated on the pool before the fee is added.

Capped method: the calculated fee is limited by a lease-defined ceiling.

The percentage is only half the instruction.

Before billing, document the base, the method, and the cap treatment if the lease has one.

A clean admin fee backup should be clear enough to audit.
