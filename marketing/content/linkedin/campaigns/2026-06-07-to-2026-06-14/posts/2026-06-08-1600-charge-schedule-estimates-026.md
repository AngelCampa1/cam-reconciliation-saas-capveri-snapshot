When the question is "what did we bill?" the GL is not enough.

For Yardi teams, Charge Schedule often becomes the support file.

It helps answer:

Which estimate charge was billed?
When did the estimate change?
Did the billed side move when the budget changed?
Which charge code carried the amount?

That matters because reconciliation is two-sided:

Adjusted actual obligation.
Estimated billings already collected.

The tenant's balance sits between those numbers.

CapVeri keeps billing support tied to the reconciliation because a correct expense pool still needs a correct credit for what was already paid.
