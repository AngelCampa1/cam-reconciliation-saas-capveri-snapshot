Pro-rata errors often hide in the denominator, not the tenant square footage.

A 12,000 RSF tenant in a 240,000 RSF building is at 5.00%.

Use 216,000 RSF as the denominator, and the same tenant jumps to 5.56%.

Nothing changed in the suite.
Nothing changed in the expense pool.
The denominator moved.

That is why CapVeri treats the lease-defined denominator as a first-class input, not a background assumption.

Total leasable area, leased area, anchor-excluded area, component area for mixed use, those are not interchangeable labels. They create different tenant shares.

The math is simple.

The source of the denominator is the control.
