BOMA 2024 adoption should not first show up on a tenant's statement.

Run the parallel calculation first.

Old measurement.
New measurement.
Tenant by tenant.
Same operating expense pool.
Same reconciliation period.

Then inspect the changes before anyone sends the package.

The source page recommends this because measurement updates can affect rentable square footage, pro-rata shares, and gross-up denominators. Even when the final change is valid, surprise creates avoidable friction.

CapVeri can use measurement inputs from a BOMA-based remeasurement, but that is not the same as claiming certification, endorsement, or a universal rule.

The defensible move is narrower:

Document the standard used, preserve the measurement support, and show how the denominator changed.
