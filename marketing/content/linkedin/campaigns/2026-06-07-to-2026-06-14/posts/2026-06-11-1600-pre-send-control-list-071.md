The pre-send CAM checklist is not a formality.

It is where small mistakes stop becoming tenant-facing statements.

A useful control list asks:

Did CapEx stay out of the recoverable pool?
Were fixed and variable expenses split before gross-up?
Does the gross-up target match the lease?
Does the pro-rata denominator match the lease?
Were partial-year changes time-weighted?
Do capped tenants use the right cap structure?
Is the management fee calculated once, on the right base?
Did late invoices make it into the right year?

None of those questions needs a speech.

They need evidence in the file before statements go out.
