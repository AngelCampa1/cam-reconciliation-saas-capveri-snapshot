Before a BOMA 2024 measurement update reaches billing, run it twice.

Once with the current measurements.

Once with the updated measurements.

Then compare tenant by tenant.

The point is not to turn a measurement standard into a dispute.

The point is to know what changed before a statement goes out.

Useful backup includes the old rentable SF, new rentable SF, denominator movement, pro-rata impact, and the lease language that controls whether the update applies now.

No surprise measurement changes.

No mystery denominator.

No certification claim needed.
