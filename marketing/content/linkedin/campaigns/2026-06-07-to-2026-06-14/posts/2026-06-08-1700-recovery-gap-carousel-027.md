Carousel outline: A recovery gap is not one thing

Slide 1: Low recovery is a question
- Not a conclusion.

Slide 2: GL mapping gap
- Expense exists, but the recovery pool did not pick it up.

Slide 3: Gross-up gap
- Variable expenses were not normalized below the lease threshold.

Slide 4: Cap constraint
- The lease may limit what can be billed.

Slide 5: Classification noise
- Recoverable, excluded, capital, and landlord-only costs got mixed.

Slide 6: Denominator drift
- Tenant shares changed because the denominator moved.

Slide 7: The useful output
- A reason code, not a vague leakage number.

Slide 8: The CapVeri view
- Model the gap before calling it recoverable.
