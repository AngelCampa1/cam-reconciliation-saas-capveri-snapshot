"15% admin fee" is not a complete calculation instruction.

It still leaves the important question:

15% of what?

Gross method: fee is part of the base, so the calculation is circular.

Net method: fee is added on top of the base, so the math is direct.

Capped method: the fee is calculated, then limited by a dollar or per-SF ceiling.

On a $500,000 base with a 15% fee, gross and net do not land in the same place.

That is why CapVeri does not treat the admin fee as one generic line item. The method has to come from the lease, and the worksheet has to show the base.

The dispute rarely starts with the percentage.

It starts with the base.
