A tenant dispute letter should not send the accounting team on a scavenger hunt.

The support packet should already have the basics:

General ledger with chart of accounts.
Gross-up worksheet with occupancy support.
Management fee base and lease clause.
Vendor invoices by month and category.
Rent roll or occupancy records.
Lease abstract with exclusions, caps, admin fees, and audit rights.

That list is not glamorous. It is the difference between answering a question and rebuilding the year under pressure.

CapVeri's stance is simple: reconciliation is not finished when the statement total is calculated. It is finished when the number can be defended from source records.
