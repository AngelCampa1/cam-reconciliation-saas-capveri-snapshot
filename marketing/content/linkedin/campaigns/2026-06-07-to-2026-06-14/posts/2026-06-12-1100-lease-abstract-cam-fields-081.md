A CAM-ready lease abstract needs more than tenant name, suite, and rent.

For reconciliation, the fields that matter are smaller and easier to miss:

Expense inclusions.
Expense exclusions.
Gross-up clause.
Occupancy target.
Pro-rata denominator.
Cap type.
Base year.
Admin fee method.
Audit window.
Statement delivery deadline.

Those fields decide how the export data gets treated.

If they live only in the PDF lease, each reconciliation can become a fresh reading project.

CapVeri works better when the abstract carries the calculation rules directly into the review file.
