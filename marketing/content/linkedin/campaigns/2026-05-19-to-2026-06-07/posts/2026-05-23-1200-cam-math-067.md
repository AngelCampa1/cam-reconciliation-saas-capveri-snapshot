A review of CapEx vs. OpEx belongs in CAM close before final billing.

1. Start with the lease rule. Capital projects need separate recoverability review before they enter a CAM pool.

2. Check the source data. Look for capitalization clues before the line enters a recoverable pool.

3. Run the calculation in a way someone else can follow. A repairs account can include invoices that deserve a capital-project check.

4. Save the support. Document the recoverability decision before the line reaches the tenant statement.

The earlier review happens, the fewer people have to touch the packet.

The best version of this review is boring and repeatable.
