Deterministic CAM math belongs in review before the packet goes out.

Input: financial math should be deterministic and auditable.

Review step: use rules, formulas, and source-backed inputs for allocations.

Common miss: AI can help read a document, but the charge calculation should come from explicit lease terms.

Approval trail: Keep calculation logic separate from document extraction.

Final check: A plausible answer is not enough when money is being billed to tenants.

If the input came from an ERP export, keep the report settings with it.
