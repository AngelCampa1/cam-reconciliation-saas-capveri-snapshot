Audit defense packet: the CAM check to run before billing.

Start with the control question: tenant questions are easier to answer when the packet already explains the math.

Source check: include source export, lease clause reference, calculation trace, and variance notes.

Where it breaks: A tenant asking about landscaping should see the GL lines, allocation basis, and exclusion review.

Evidence to keep: GL source, lease rule, calculation input, and tenant impact.

Before billing: Build the audit trail before sending the statement.

Waiting until an auditor asks for support usually creates more work than assembling it up front.

The best version of this review is boring and repeatable.
