GL export QA belongs in review before the packet goes out.

Input: an export can balance and still be incomplete for CAM work.

Review step: check date filters, accrual timing, entity scope, and excluded accounts.

Common miss: A late-posted accrual can be outside the export even when the report total ties to the ledger run.

Approval trail: Save the export parameters with the reconciliation file.

Final check: The CSV is the evidence trail. Treat the report settings as part of the evidence.

A review-ready packet does not need drama. It needs traceable inputs.
