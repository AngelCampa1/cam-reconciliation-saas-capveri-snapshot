When property tax pass-throughs changes, the tenant note should change with it.

Setup: tax pass-throughs can move tenant charges quickly.

Drift: separate assessment changes, refunds, appeals, and prior-year adjustments.

Trace: A tax refund should be tracked back to the tenants and years affected by the original charge.

Answer: Tie the tax line to the bill, appeal status, allocation rule, and recovery period.

Tax lines are often high-dollar, so weak support gets noticed.

If the review depends on judgment, write down the judgment while it is fresh.
