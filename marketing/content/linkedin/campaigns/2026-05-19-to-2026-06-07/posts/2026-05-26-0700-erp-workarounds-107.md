The quiet risk in MRI export review starts before the statement is formatted.

What to verify: compare share type, billing pool, lease exclusions, and gross-up rules before statements go out.

What can go wrong: A tenant can be assigned to the right pool and still use the wrong denominator.

What to save: GL source, lease rule, calculation input, and tenant impact.

How to close it: Run a tenant-by-tenant exception report before final billing.

Watch item: The issue is usually configuration drift, not a broken ERP.

Capture the note before the packet becomes tenant-facing.

If a statement needs revision, this note should explain exactly where to look.
