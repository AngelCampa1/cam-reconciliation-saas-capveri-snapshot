Input: a tenant share is only as reliable as the denominator behind it.

Review: match the denominator to the lease, not just the building setup screen.

Evidence: Building RSF, occupied RSF, floor RSF, and pool RSF can all produce different percentages.

Close: Store the denominator source next to the calculation output.

A percentage with no denominator support is hard to explain after a tenant asks questions.

The reviewer should be able to see what changed since the prior run.
