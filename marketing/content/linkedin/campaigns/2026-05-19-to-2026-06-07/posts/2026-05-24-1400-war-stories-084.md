The packet is not ready until the calculation audit trail is tied back to the source.

Rule: a calculation audit trail connects each output back to its source.

Check: keep GL line, pool assignment, lease clause, factor, cap rule, and tenant allocation together.

Miss: If a tenant asks why their share changed, the answer should not require rebuilding the workbook.

Fix: Store the trace with the final packet.

A spreadsheet total without trace support is fragile evidence.

If the backup lives outside the packet, add the reference before statements go out.
