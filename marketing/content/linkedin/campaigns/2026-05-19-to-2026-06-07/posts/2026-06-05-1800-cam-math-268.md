Management fees should leave a trail a second reviewer can follow.

1. Start with the lease rule. Management fees depend on the lease basis and any fee cap.

2. Check the source data. Verify whether the fee is calculated on gross rent, recoverable expenses, or another base.

3. Run the calculation in a way someone else can follow. Applying a fee to an expense pool that already includes the fee can create circular math.

4. Save the support. Calculate the fee from the stated lease basis, then document the basis in the packet.

Management fee errors are easy to miss because the percentage itself often looks reasonable.

This check is small, but it keeps the packet from depending on memory.
