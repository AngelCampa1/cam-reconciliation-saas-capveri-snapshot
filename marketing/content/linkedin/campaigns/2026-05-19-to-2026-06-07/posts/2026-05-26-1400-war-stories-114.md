A review of the calculation audit trail belongs in CAM close before final billing.

1. Start with the lease rule. A calculation audit trail connects each output back to its source.

2. Check the source data. Keep GL line, pool assignment, lease clause, factor, cap rule, and tenant allocation together.

3. Run the calculation in a way someone else can follow. If a tenant asks why their share changed, the answer should not require rebuilding the workbook.

4. Save the support. Store the trace with the final packet.

A spreadsheet total without trace support is fragile evidence.

A tenant-level variance note is useful even when the final charge is correct.
