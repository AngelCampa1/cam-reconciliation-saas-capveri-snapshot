Start with pre-send QA.

Lease rule: the best time to catch a CAM issue is before the tenant packet goes out.

Source file: run checks for pool membership, non-recoverable lines, gross-up, caps, and denominator changes.

Tenant impact: One excluded GL account caught before billing is cleaner than a revised statement after billing.

Close file: Use a pre-send checklist every cycle.

One clear memo now can prevent a long email thread later.

A review-ready packet does not need drama. It needs traceable inputs.
