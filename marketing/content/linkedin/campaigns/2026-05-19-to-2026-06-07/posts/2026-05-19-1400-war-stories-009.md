Tenant disputes deserves a source check before anyone formats the packet.

Setup: many disputes start with missing support, not a dramatic accounting error.

Drift: review the lines tenants are most likely to question before the packet is sent.

Trace: Taxes, insurance, management fees, CapEx, and administrative charges need especially clear support.

Answer: Attach the support while the reconciliation is still fresh.

A vague explanation can make a correct charge look suspect.

If the issue repeats next year, the note should still make sense.
