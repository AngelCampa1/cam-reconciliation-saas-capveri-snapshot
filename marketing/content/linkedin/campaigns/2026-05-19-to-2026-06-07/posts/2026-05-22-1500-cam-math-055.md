Gross-up factor deserves a source check before anyone formats the packet.

Input: gross-up should apply to variable expenses only.

Review: compare actual occupancy to the lease target before applying a factor.

Evidence: If actual occupancy is already at or above the target, the factor should be 1.0.

Close: Separate fixed and variable expenses before the reconciliation run.

Keep gross-up limited to the expense categories the lease and review file support.

This is the kind of check that should happen before statement formatting starts.
