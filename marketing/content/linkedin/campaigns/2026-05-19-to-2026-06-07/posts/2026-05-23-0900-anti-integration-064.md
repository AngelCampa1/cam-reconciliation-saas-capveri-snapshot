Start with spreadsheet risk.

Lease rule: Excel is flexible, but flexibility can hide broken assumptions.

Source file: check hard-coded factors, copied formulas, hidden tabs, and stale denominators.

Tenant impact: A workbook can pass tie-out while one tenant row still points to last year's denominator.

Close file: Move repeated reconciliation logic into a traceable workflow.

The reviewer needs enough detail to approve the charge without guessing.

If the issue repeats next year, the note should still make sense.
