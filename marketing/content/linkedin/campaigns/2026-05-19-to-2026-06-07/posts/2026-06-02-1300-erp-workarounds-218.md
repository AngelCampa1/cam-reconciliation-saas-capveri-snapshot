Yardi export review is a small review step that can change the tenant answer.

Rule: Yardi can be the system of record without being the only calculation check.

Check: review recovery pool, share type, gross-up, cap, and exclusion settings against the lease.

Miss: The report can run cleanly while one tenant uses the wrong share type.

Fix: Export the data, recalculate independently, then compare.

A correct ERP report means the system followed its setup. It does not by itself show the setup matches the lease.

Use this as a first-pass control, then move exceptions into a separate queue.
