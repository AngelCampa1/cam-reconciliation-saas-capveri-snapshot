Spreadsheet risk belongs in review before the packet goes out.

Input: Excel is flexible, but flexibility can hide broken assumptions.

Review step: check hard-coded factors, copied formulas, hidden tabs, and stale denominators.

Common miss: A workbook can pass tie-out while one tenant row still points to last year's denominator.

Approval trail: Move repeated reconciliation logic into a traceable workflow.

Final check: The risk is not that spreadsheets are bad. The risk is that they are too easy to trust after they tie out.

The approval path matters as much as the formula when the number is questioned.
