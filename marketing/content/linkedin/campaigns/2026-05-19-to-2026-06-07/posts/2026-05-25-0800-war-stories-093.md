The quiet risk in the audit defense packet starts before the statement is formatted.

What to verify: include source export, lease clause reference, calculation trace, and variance notes.

What can go wrong: A tenant asking about landscaping should see the GL lines, allocation basis, and exclusion review.

What to save: GL source, lease rule, calculation input, and tenant impact.

How to close it: Build the audit trail before sending the statement.

Watch item: Waiting until an auditor asks for support usually creates more work than assembling it up front.

The reviewer needs enough detail to approve the charge without guessing.

The close file should show both the accounting source and the lease logic.
