Property tax pass-throughs belongs in review before the packet goes out.

Input: tax pass-throughs can move tenant charges quickly.

Review step: separate assessment changes, refunds, appeals, and prior-year adjustments.

Common miss: A tax refund should be tracked back to the tenants and years affected by the original charge.

Approval trail: Tie the tax line to the bill, appeal status, allocation rule, and recovery period.

Final check: Tax lines are often high-dollar, so weak support gets noticed.

If the issue repeats next year, the note should still make sense.
