A review of the base-year stop belongs in CAM close before final billing.

1. Start with the lease rule. Base-year leases compare current recoverable expenses against a baseline year.

2. Check the source data. Normalize one-time items before using the base year as a benchmark.

3. Run the calculation in a way someone else can follow. Storm cleanup in the base year should not quietly lower recoveries for every later year.

4. Save the support. Review the base-year pool before current-year allocations.

A messy base year makes every future reconciliation look mathematically correct and economically wrong.

A tenant-level variance note is useful even when the final charge is correct.
