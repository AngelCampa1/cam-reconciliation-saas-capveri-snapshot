Before you send the CAM packet, check tenant dispute prep.

Source: many disputes start with missing support, not a dramatic accounting error.

Lease: review the lines tenants are most likely to question before the packet is sent.

Exception: Taxes, insurance, management fees, CapEx, and administrative charges need especially clear support.

Packet: Attach the support while the reconciliation is still fresh.

A vague explanation can make a correct charge look suspect.

If the input came from an ERP export, keep the report settings with it.
