The quiet risk in deterministic CAM math starts before the statement is formatted.

What to verify: use rules, formulas, and source-backed inputs for allocations.

What can go wrong: AI can help read a document, but the charge calculation should come from explicit lease terms.

What to save: GL source, lease rule, calculation input, and tenant impact.

How to close it: Keep calculation logic separate from document extraction.

Watch item: A plausible answer is not enough when money is being billed to tenants.

Capture the note before the packet becomes tenant-facing.

If the issue repeats next year, the note should still make sense.
