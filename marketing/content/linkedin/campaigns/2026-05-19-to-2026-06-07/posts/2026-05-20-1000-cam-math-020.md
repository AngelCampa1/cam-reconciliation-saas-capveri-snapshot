Before you send the CAM packet, check the gross-up factor.

Setup: gross-up should apply to variable expenses only.

Drift: compare actual occupancy to the lease target before applying a factor.

Trace: If actual occupancy is already at or above the target, the factor should be 1.0.

Answer: Separate fixed and variable expenses before the reconciliation run.

Keep gross-up limited to the expense categories the lease and review file support.

Use this check to find setup drift before it becomes tenant-facing.
