BOMA 2024 belongs in review before the packet goes out.

Input: measurement changes can affect pro-rata shares and billing support.

Review step: review lease language before applying a new measurement standard.

Common miss: A lease tied to a specific BOMA version may not behave like a lease that says standards as amended.

Approval trail: Model old and new denominators before updating the billing system.

Final check: Do not let a measurement update become a silent billing change.

A missing source note can create the same delay as a wrong formula.
