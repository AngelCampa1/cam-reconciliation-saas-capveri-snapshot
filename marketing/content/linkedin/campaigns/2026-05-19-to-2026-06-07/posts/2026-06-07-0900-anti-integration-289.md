Spreadsheet risk: the CAM check to run before billing.

Start with the control question: Excel is flexible, but flexibility can hide broken assumptions.

Source check: check hard-coded factors, copied formulas, hidden tabs, and stale denominators.

Where it breaks: A workbook can pass tie-out while one tenant row still points to last year's denominator.

Evidence to keep: GL source, lease rule, calculation input, and tenant impact.

Before billing: Move repeated reconciliation logic into a traceable workflow.

The risk is not that spreadsheets are bad. The risk is that they are too easy to trust after they tie out.

Keep the final answer tied to the exact export used for billing.
