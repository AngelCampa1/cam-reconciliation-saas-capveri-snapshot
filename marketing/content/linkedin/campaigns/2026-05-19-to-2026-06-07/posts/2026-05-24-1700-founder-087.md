Start with the controller workflow.

Lease rule: controllers need exception visibility before they need prettier statements.

Source file: prioritize what changed, what failed, what needs approval, and what is ready to send.

Tenant impact: A tenant with a denominator change belongs in an exception queue, not buried in a PDF packet.

Close file: Design the workflow around review decisions.

Treat the exception note as part of the reconciliation, not an afterthought.

Use this as a first-pass control, then move exceptions into a separate queue.
