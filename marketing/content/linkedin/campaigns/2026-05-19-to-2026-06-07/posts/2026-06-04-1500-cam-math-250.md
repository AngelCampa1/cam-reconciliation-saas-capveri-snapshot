The quiet risk in management fees starts before the statement is formatted.

What to verify: verify whether the fee is calculated on gross rent, recoverable expenses, or another base.

What can go wrong: Applying a fee to an expense pool that already includes the fee can create circular math.

What to save: GL source, lease rule, calculation input, and tenant impact.

How to close it: Calculate the fee from the stated lease basis, then document the basis in the packet.

Watch item: Management fee errors are easy to miss because the percentage itself often looks reasonable.

A controller should be able to review this without reopening last year's workbook.

A cleaner packet gives the property team fewer follow-up questions to answer.
