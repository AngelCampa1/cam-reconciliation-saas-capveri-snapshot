Base-year stop deserves a source check before anyone formats the packet.

Setup: base-year leases compare current recoverable expenses against a baseline year.

Drift: normalize one-time items before using the base year as a benchmark.

Trace: Storm cleanup in the base year should not quietly lower recoveries for every later year.

Answer: Review the base-year pool before current-year allocations.

A messy base year makes every future reconciliation look mathematically correct and economically wrong.

Keep the backup simple enough for someone outside accounting to follow.
