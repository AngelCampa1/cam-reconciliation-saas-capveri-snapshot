Start with the CAM close calendar.

Lease rule: CAM close works better when review steps are scheduled before statement drafting.

Source file: reserve time for export QA, lease-rule review, exception resolution, and packet assembly.

Tenant impact: Do not discover missing lease abstracts after the GL has already been finalized.

Close file: Treat CAM close like a finance close, with controls and owners.

If the answer depends on a hidden workbook tab, move the support into the close file.

The close file should show both the accounting source and the lease logic.
