A clean close file shows how the pro-rata denominator moved from source to tenant answer.

Setup: a tenant share is only as reliable as the denominator behind it.

Drift: match the denominator to the lease, not just the building setup screen.

Trace: Building RSF, occupied RSF, floor RSF, and pool RSF can all produce different percentages.

Answer: Store the denominator source next to the calculation output.

A percentage with no denominator support is hard to explain after a tenant asks questions.

This check is small, but it keeps the packet from depending on memory.
