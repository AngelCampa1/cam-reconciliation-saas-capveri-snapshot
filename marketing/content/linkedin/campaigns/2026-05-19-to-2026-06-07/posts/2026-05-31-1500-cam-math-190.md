The quiet risk in the pro-rata denominator starts before the statement is formatted.

What to verify: match the denominator to the lease, not just the building setup screen.

What can go wrong: Building RSF, occupied RSF, floor RSF, and pool RSF can all produce different percentages.

What to save: GL source, lease rule, calculation input, and tenant impact.

How to close it: Store the denominator source next to the calculation output.

Watch item: A percentage with no denominator support is hard to explain after a tenant asks questions.

Capture the note before the packet becomes tenant-facing.

A tenant-level variance note is useful even when the final charge is correct.
