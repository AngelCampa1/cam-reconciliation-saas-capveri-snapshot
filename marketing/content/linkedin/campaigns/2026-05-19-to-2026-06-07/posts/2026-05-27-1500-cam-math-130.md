A clean close file shows how CAM caps moved from source to tenant answer.

Input: caps only work when the base amount and cap type are recorded correctly.

Review: confirm whether each cap is cumulative, non-cumulative, compounded, or fixed.

Evidence: A 5 percent cap can behave very differently depending on whether unused cap room carries forward.

Close: Keep a cap ledger by tenant and year.

A cap field without the cap type is not enough support for a tenant packet.

When the rule changes by tenant, do not let the portfolio default hide it.
