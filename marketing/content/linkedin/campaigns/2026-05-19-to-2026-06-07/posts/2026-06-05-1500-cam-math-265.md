The quiet risk in CapEx vs. OpEx starts before the statement is formatted.

What to verify: look for capitalization clues before the line enters a recoverable pool.

What can go wrong: A repairs account can include invoices that deserve a capital-project check.

What to save: GL source, lease rule, calculation input, and tenant impact.

How to close it: Document the recoverability decision before the line reaches the tenant statement.

Watch item: A GL account called repairs is not proof that every line is recoverable maintenance.

If the answer depends on a hidden workbook tab, move the support into the close file.

The close file should show both the accounting source and the lease logic.
