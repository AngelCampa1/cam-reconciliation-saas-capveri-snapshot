When CAM caps changes, the tenant note should change with it.

Setup: caps only work when the base amount and cap type are recorded correctly.

Drift: confirm whether each cap is cumulative, non-cumulative, compounded, or fixed.

Trace: A 5 percent cap can behave very differently depending on whether unused cap room carries forward.

Answer: Keep a cap ledger by tenant and year.

A cap field without the cap type is not enough support for a tenant packet.

If the backup lives outside the packet, add the reference before statements go out.
