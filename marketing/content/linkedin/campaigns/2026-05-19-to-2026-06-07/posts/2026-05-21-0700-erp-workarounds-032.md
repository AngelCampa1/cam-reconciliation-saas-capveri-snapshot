Start with GL export QA.

Lease rule: an export can balance and still be incomplete for CAM work.

Source file: check date filters, accrual timing, entity scope, and excluded accounts.

Tenant impact: A late-posted accrual can be outside the export even when the report total ties to the ledger run.

Close file: Save the export parameters with the reconciliation file.

A controller should be able to review this without reopening last year's workbook.

A tenant-level variance note is useful even when the final charge is correct.
