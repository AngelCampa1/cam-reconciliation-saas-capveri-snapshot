BOMA 2024 deserves a source check before anyone formats the packet.

Setup: measurement changes can affect pro-rata shares and billing support.

Drift: review lease language before applying a new measurement standard.

Trace: A lease tied to a specific BOMA version may not behave like a lease that says standards as amended.

Answer: Model old and new denominators before updating the billing system.

Do not let a measurement update become a silent billing change.

The close file should show both the accounting source and the lease logic.
