The quiet risk in export-based verification starts before the statement is formatted.

What to verify: use CSV, Excel, and PDF outputs as the input layer.

What can go wrong: Keep Yardi or MRI as the system of record, then run an independent lease-term calculation beside it.

What to save: GL source, lease rule, calculation input, and tenant impact.

How to close it: Verify the output before changing the system.

Watch item: The integration is not the proof. The calculation trace is the proof.

A controller should be able to review this without reopening last year's workbook.

If the backup lives outside the packet, add the reference before statements go out.
