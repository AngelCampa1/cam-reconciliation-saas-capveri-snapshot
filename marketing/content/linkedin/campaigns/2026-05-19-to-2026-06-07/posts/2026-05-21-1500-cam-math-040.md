The quiet risk in CAM caps starts before the statement is formatted.

What to verify: confirm whether each cap is cumulative, non-cumulative, compounded, or fixed.

What can go wrong: A 5 percent cap can behave very differently depending on whether unused cap room carries forward.

What to save: GL source, lease rule, calculation input, and tenant impact.

How to close it: Keep a cap ledger by tenant and year.

Watch item: A cap field without the cap type is not enough support for a tenant packet.

One clear memo now can prevent a long email thread later.

If the same exception appears twice, make it a control next year.
