A review of pre-send QA belongs in CAM close before final billing.

1. Start with the lease rule. The best time to catch a CAM issue is before the tenant packet goes out.

2. Check the source data. Run checks for pool membership, non-recoverable lines, gross-up, caps, and denominator changes.

3. Run the calculation in a way someone else can follow. One excluded GL account caught before billing is cleaner than a revised statement after billing.

4. Save the support. Use a pre-send checklist every cycle.

The best exception queue is short, specific, and resolved before billing.

If the review depends on judgment, write down the judgment while it is fresh.
