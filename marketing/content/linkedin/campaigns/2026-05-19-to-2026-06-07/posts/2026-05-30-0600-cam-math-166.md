CAM teams do not need more ceremony. They need better checks around CAM caps.

1. Start with the lease rule. Caps only work when the base amount and cap type are recorded correctly.

2. Check the source data. Confirm whether each cap is cumulative, non-cumulative, compounded, or fixed.

3. Run the calculation in a way someone else can follow. A 5 percent cap can behave very differently depending on whether unused cap room carries forward.

4. Save the support. Keep a cap ledger by tenant and year.

A tie-out proves totals. It does not by itself show recoverability.

The cleanest workflow keeps the source export and lease rule beside the output.
