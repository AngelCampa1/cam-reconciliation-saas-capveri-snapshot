Pre-send QA belongs in review before the packet goes out.

Input: the best time to catch a CAM issue is before the tenant packet goes out.

Review step: run checks for pool membership, non-recoverable lines, gross-up, caps, and denominator changes.

Common miss: One excluded GL account caught before billing is cleaner than a revised statement after billing.

Approval trail: Use a pre-send checklist every cycle.

Final check: A signed reconciliation packet should not be the first time the math is reviewed end to end.

The cleanest workflow keeps the source export and lease rule beside the output.
