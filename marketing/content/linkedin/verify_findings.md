### Panko / EuSpRIG "88% of corporate spreadsheet models contain errors"
- **Actual finding (Panko, 1998 via EuSpRIG):** 88% of *manual interventions* in End-User Computing contain errors - NOT 88% of corporate spreadsheets.
- Better-supported figure from field audits: "at least 86% of audited spreadsheets had errors" and an average cell error rate of 3.9% that compounds with sheet size.
- The "88%" figure also appears in other research re: "88 spreadsheets had a 94% error rate" - different study, different number.
- Sources:
  - [Panko: What We Know About Spreadsheet Errors](http://panko.shidler.hawaii.edu/SSR/Mypapers/EUSPRIG_2000.htm)
  - [EuSpRIG papers](https://eusprig.org/)
- **Action:** revise. Change "88% of corporate spreadsheet models" → "at least 86% of audited corporate spreadsheets contain errors per Raymond Panko's research via EuSpRIG" OR rephrase to "the majority of corporate spreadsheets contain errors, with average cell error rates around 3.9% that compound at scale (Panko / EuSpRIG)".

### Marsh / Lockton "insurance premiums increased 15-25% in 2024"
- **Actual reports (2024):** Lockton's Q4 2023→2024 outlook showed commercial property premium changes ranging from -5% to +5% for noncatastrophe exposed, -10% to +10% for catastrophe exposed. Marsh's Global Insurance Market Index showed Q4 2023 US property rates +11% (decelerating from earlier 14-19% increases).
- The 15-25% figure is the higher end of *2022-2023* data, NOT 2024. Citing it as 2024 is incorrect.
- Sources:
  - [Marsh MMA 2025 Property Insurance Trends](https://www.marshmma.com/us/insights/details/property-insurance-trends.html)
  - [Lockton Market Update](https://global.lockton.com/us/en/lmu)
  - [Business Insurance: Commercial property prices trending down (Lockton)](https://www.businessinsurance.com/commercial-property-prices-trending-down-casualty-up-lockton/)
- **Action:** revise. Either (a) re-date to "2022-2023 cycle per Marsh / Lockton market reports" with the 15-25% range preserved, or (b) keep "2024" but revise to "single-digit increases on average, with catastrophe-exposed properties seeing higher renewals" per Lockton 2024 outlook.
