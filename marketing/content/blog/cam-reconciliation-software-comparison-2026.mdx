In 2026, the market splits into two distinct categories: **ERP platforms** that handle end-to-end property management including CAM billing, and **dedicated verification tools** that independently audit CAM calculations for accuracy. The best stack for most commercial landlords uses both.

## Key Features of CAM Reconciliation Software

The most effective platforms share four core capabilities:

- **Automation:** Automatically calculates pro-rata shares based on square footage or lease terms, applies gross-up adjustments, and enforces CAM caps without manual spreadsheet work
- **Integration or Import:** Connects directly with accounting systems (via API) or imports ERP GL exports (CSV/Excel) to pull actual expense data
- **Tenant Portals:** Provides transparent reporting portals where tenants can review reconciliation statements, reducing dispute volume by giving tenants self-service access to supporting documentation
- **Audit Trails:** Generates line-by-line documentation of every calculation step, essential for defending against tenant audit requests and proving compliance with lease terms

## Nakisa Real Estate (NRE)

Nakisa Real Estate focuses on automating both location-level CAM costs and lease-level reconciliations for enterprise commercial portfolios. The platform handles two reconciliation levels simultaneously: property-wide expense allocation and individual lease-level true-up calculation.

**Best for:** Large enterprise portfolios (50+ properties) with complex multi-entity structures and existing Yardi or MRI installations.

**Strengths:** Handles multi-currency, multi-entity, and cross-border portfolios; strong integration with SAP and Oracle for GL data; sophisticated allocation logic for mixed-use properties.

**Limitations:** Requires implementation engagement (typically 3–6 months); priced for enterprise budgets; overkill for portfolios under 20 properties.

## MRI Software

MRI's CAM module, part of MRI Commercial Management, uses machine-learning-assisted lease abstraction for faster recovery processing. Particularly strong for office-heavy portfolios requiring floor-level CAM allocation across multi-story buildings.

**Best for:** Office-dominant portfolios with complex multi-story allocation requirements and existing MRI installations.

**Strengths:** Superior multi-story floor-level allocation; sophisticated share type configurations; strong multi-entity ownership support.

**Limitations:** Steep learning curve; configuration complexity requires dedicated MRI administrators; same self-verification gap as all ERPs. It calculates based on configuration, not lease terms.

## Kardin Systems

Kardin specializes in simplifying expense report comparisons and CAM documentation storage for commercial landlords. The Kardin Portal focuses on making the year-end reconciliation process manageable for property management teams that don't have a full ERP.

**Best for:** Mid-market landlords (10–50 properties) without a full ERP deployment who need dedicated CAM management without the complexity of Yardi or MRI.

**Strengths:** Purpose-built for CAM reconciliation (not adapted from a broader ERP); straightforward expense comparison workflow; good documentation storage.

**Limitations:** Less suited for portfolios that already have Yardi or MRI in place; not designed as a verification layer on top of existing ERP output.

## Rent Manager

Rent Manager enables comparing commercial recoverable expenses (CRE) to tenant CAM charges, with functionality covering retail, office, and industrial properties. Works for mixed commercial/residential portfolios.

**Best for:** Portfolios managing both commercial and residential assets in a single platform; smaller commercial portfolios (under 20 properties).

**Strengths:** Strong commercial/residential crossover functionality; accessible pricing; built-in CAM reconciliation feature within a full property management platform.

**Limitations:** CAM functionality is secondary to property management; less sophisticated gross-up and cap logic compared to enterprise CAM-specific tools.

## Re-Leased

Re-Leased focuses on automated CAM allocation and real-time workflows for commercial property management. Creates, tracks, and reconciles CAM budgets with integrations to accounting systems.

**Best for:** Commercial-focused landlords wanting a modern cloud platform with strong accounting integrations (Xero, QuickBooks, Sage).

**Strengths:** Clean modern interface; strong accounting system integrations; real-time budget vs. actual tracking throughout the year.

**Limitations:** Less suited for U.S. BOMA 2024 gross-up requirements; best for straightforward lease structures without complex cap logic.

## Yardi Breeze Premier

Yardi Breeze Premier offers solid CAM functionality including "amount per area" charging for smaller commercial portfolios. Charges individual tenants based on their square footage automatically, making it more accessible than Yardi Voyager for smaller operations.

**Best for:** Small-to-mid commercial portfolios (under 20 properties) already using Yardi for residential management, or new commercial operators wanting an all-in-one platform.

**Strengths:** Significantly lower cost than Yardi Voyager; familiar Yardi interface; per-unit pricing with no large upfront commitment; accessible CAM setup.

**Limitations:** Less sophisticated than Yardi Voyager for complex lease structures; limited audit trail depth; no independent verification of its own calculations.

## Two Categories: ERP vs. Verification

The fundamental distinction matters for software evaluation:

**ERP CAM Modules** (Yardi, MRI, Nakisa, Rent Manager, Re-Leased) calculate what you've configured them to calculate. They don't verify that their configuration matches your current lease terms. Configuration drift (when lease amendments aren't reflected in ERP settings) is the primary source of systematic CAM billing errors.

**Dedicated Verification Tools** (CapVeri) recalculate independently from raw GL data and current lease terms, then compare against ERP output. They don't replace ERPs. They verify them.

For most mid-market commercial landlords, the optimal stack is: your existing ERP for property management and billing, plus independent verification software to catch errors before statements leave your office.
