Problem and buying case

The core problem: recoverable revenue is trapped in fragile workflows.

Most commercial landlords already have the data. The failure point is the control layer around it: amended lease terms, expense pools, occupancy denominators, cap logic, base-year treatment, compliance rules, dispute records, and spreadsheet formulas drift away from what the lease actually allows.

What breaks
  • Gross-up factors applied to fixed expenses that should not be grossed up.
  • Caps calculated from the wrong base year, cap type, or amendment version.
  • Pro-rata shares that miss mid-year move-ins, expansions, contractions, or denominator changes.
  • Errors that compound year after year because old workbook logic is copied forward.
  • GL anomalies, CapEx screening, tax normalization, compliance exports, and tenant audit packets handled manually after risk appears.
