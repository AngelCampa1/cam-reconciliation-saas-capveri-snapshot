# AI transparency statement

**CapVeri** | Last updated: 2026-05-28

This statement explains how artificial intelligence is used in CapVeri, what data is sent to AI systems, and what controls are in place. It is consistent with the disclosure requirements of the Colorado Artificial Intelligence Act (CAIA, effective February 1, 2026) and gives customers a clear picture of AI's role in the product.

---

## What AI does in CapVeri

AI is used for document understanding and review assistance. The primary function is extracting structured financial terms from lease documents; AI is also used to assist with anomaly review of general-ledger data. All financial math remains deterministic Python (see *What AI does not do*).

When you upload a lease PDF, the document goes through this pipeline:

1. The lease PDF (and/or its extracted text) is sent to AI models via OpenRouter with a structured prompt. Two sibling models extract independently and a third "judge" model arbitrates disagreements (dual-extraction). A gap-filler step may re-request specific missing fields.
2. The models return specific fields: pro-rata share, base year, base year amount, expense pool exclusions, cap type, cap rate, and admin fee percentage
3. Every extracted field goes to a human verification screen before it can affect any calculation

AI is additionally used for:
- Cross-document checks (comparing lease terms against statements)
- Anomaly analysis over aggregated general-ledger data (flagging unusual entries for human review; this does not perform reconciliation math)
- Pool-matching classification

All models are accessed through OpenRouter, which routes to configured downstream providers (Google Gemini, Moonshot Kimi, OpenAI GPT, and Z.ai GLM). CapVeri does not use a direct Anthropic or OpenAI client.

---

## What AI does not do

- No CAM reconciliation calculations — all financial math is deterministic Python using `decimal` types
- No billing decisions
- No lease interpretation for legal purposes

Note: AI **is** used to flag potential anomalies in aggregated GL data for human review, but it does not compute reconciliation results. If you are wondering whether a specific output was generated by AI: reconciliation numbers, pool allocations, tenant shares, and cap calculations are not AI-generated. They come from the calculation engine.

---

## Human review is always required

Every AI extraction goes through a mandatory verification screen before any extracted value can affect a reconciliation. This is not optional and cannot be bypassed.

The verification screen shows:

- Each extracted field with its value
- The specific text quote from the lease that the model cited as the source (source attribution)
- A confidence score (0–100) for each field
- Fields below 50% confidence are flagged and cannot be accepted without active confirmation

Users must review and confirm (or correct) each field. The system records whether a value was accepted as-is or corrected by the reviewer.

---

## Data sent to AI

- The uploaded lease document (as a PDF and/or extracted text) for lease extraction.
- Aggregated general-ledger data (account-level totals and flagged anomalies) for the GL anomaly-analysis step.

We do not send to AI providers:
- Stripe payment details or card data
- Account credentials
- Personally identifiable information beyond what appears in the documents themselves

---

## AI model and configuration

**Provider/gateway**: OpenRouter (openrouter.ai), routing to configured downstream models — Google Gemini, Moonshot Kimi, OpenAI GPT, and Z.ai GLM
**Temperature**: 0.0 for extraction, judging, gap-fill, and GL analysis (cross-document checks run at 0.1)
**Purpose**: Structured data extraction and review assistance

Low temperature is used specifically to reduce hallucination risk. The models are asked to locate and return specific clauses from a document, not to reason or generate freeform content.

---

## Data retention by AI providers

Document text and data sent to OpenRouter for processing are subject to OpenRouter's and the downstream model providers' data-handling policies. See openrouter.ai for current terms.

CapVeri retains extracted fields and raw OCR output as part of the document record, subject to the retention periods in the Data Retention Policy.

## Requesting manual-only processing

Any customer can request that their account be processed without AI extraction. Manual entry of all lease financial terms is fully supported. Contact angel.campa@capveri.com to enable this for your account.

## Colorado AI Act (CAIA) disclosure

CapVeri uses AI to assist with consequential financial decisions (CAM reconciliation affects charges billed to commercial tenants).

Consistent with CAIA requirements:

1. AI output is clearly identified as AI-assisted in the verification interface
2. A human reviews and confirms all AI extractions before they affect any calculation
3. Customers may request manual processing at any time (contact angel.campa@capveri.com)
4. This statement is provided upon request and linked from the product
