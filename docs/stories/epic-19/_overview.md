# Epic 19: Tenant Portal

## Purpose
Provide tenants with read-only access to view their CAM reconciliation bills, supporting documentation, and payment history.

## Business Value
Reduces landlord support burden by giving tenants self-service access to billing information. Improves transparency, builds trust, and provides a professional experience that differentiates CapVeri from competitors.

## Dependencies
- Epic 7 (Reconciliation API) - Snapshot data
- Epic 13 (Reporting & Export UI) - PDF generation
