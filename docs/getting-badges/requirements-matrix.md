# Submission Requirements Matrix

All requirements below were checked against public platform guidance on May 15, 2026. No submission
was ever made to any of these platforms, so no "CapVeri fit" assessment below was ever tested.

Two assessments were wrong when written, and both are corrected in place: CapVeri never had a
reachable website on `capveri.com`, and it never had a signup or trial a visitor could reach.
Several platforms below require exactly those things.

## SaaSHub

**Verified requirements**

- Accepts SaaS, PaaS, IaaS, software products, apps with decent websites, and niche-leading services.
- Rejects unreleased products, waitlist-only landing pages, free-subdomain products, non-English products, and agencies.
- Recommends adding categories and competitors.
- Domain email verification gives higher priority.

**CapVeri fit**

- Assessed at the time as a good fit, "released English B2B SaaS on `capveri.com`". **Wrong on both
  counts** — it was not released, and `capveri.com` served nothing. SaaSHub rejects unreleased
  products.
- Use Finance, Accounting, Financial Reporting, Commercial Real Estate Software, or Lease Administration if available.
- Avoid CRM categories unless the form has no closer real estate/accounting option.
- Add competitors to avoid queue slowdown.

**Owner-only step**

- Login, submit, and verify with a `capveri.com` email.

**Source**

- https://www.saashub.com/services/submit

## AlternativeTo

**Verified requirements**

- Account needed.
- Profiles show product name, short description, official links, cost/license, platforms, application type, screenshots, features, tags, and alternatives.
- The login FAQ confirms account creation via major social login or email/password.

**CapVeri fit**

- Good fit as online SaaS, proprietary, paid with a free trial.
- Best framed as an alternative to Excel/manual reconciliation and a complement to Yardi/MRI.

**Owner-only step**

- Create an account and add or suggest the app from the signed-in flow.

**Source**

- https://alternativeto.net/
- https://blog.alternativeto.net/posts/login-faq/

## Product Hunt

**Verified requirements**

- Submit from a personal account.
- URL cannot be shortened or tracked.
- Product name only.
- Tagline max 60 characters.
- Description max 500 characters.
- Choose up to 3 launch tags.
- Required square thumbnail, recommended 240 x 240, under 3MB.
- Requires 2 or more gallery images, recommended 1270 x 760.
- Optional YouTube video.
- Pricing choices are free, paid, or paid with free trial/plan.
- First maker comment is important.
- Do not ask for upvotes directly.
- Can schedule up to 1 month ahead.
- Same-product launches inside six months require Product Hunt's relaunch/update path.

**CapVeri fit**

- Good fit only as a coordinated launch.
- The audience is broader than CRE, so copy should explain the painful niche quickly.

**Owner-only step**

- Login with maker account, confirm no Product Hunt launch in the past six months, add maker usernames, choose launch date/time, upload assets, and comment live.

**Source**

- https://www.producthunt.com/launch/preparing-for-launch

## G2

**Verified requirements**

- B2B software only.
- Rejects B2C products, duplicate listings, extensions/integrations as standalone listings, and inaccessible websites.
- One profile per separately sold product.
- Categories are based on functionality and inclusion criteria.
- Lease Administration requires lease data storage, financial analysis/reporting, and custom critical date alarms.
- Real Estate Activities Management is agency/activity-management oriented and requires activity monitoring that CapVeri should not claim without evidence.
- Profile logo must be at least 400px in JPG, PNG, or GIF.
- Grid logo must be SVG under 5MB.
- Banner image must be JPG, PNG, or GIF under 5MB, recommended 1260 x 240 or 2500 x 476.
- Product overview supports up to 500 words.

**CapVeri fit**

- Good fit as B2B software.
- Do not request Real Estate Activities Management unless CapVeri can prove agent/client activity monitoring and the other G2 criteria.
- Request Lease Administration only if custom critical date alarms are confirmed.
- If G2 has no accurate existing category, submit a category-creation request for CAM Reconciliation Software with competitor and functionality evidence.

**Owner-only step**

- Confirm no existing listing, confirm category evidence, submit product, claim listing after approval, and complete legal/seller profile fields.

**Source**

- https://research.g2.com/methodology/research-faq
- https://documentation.g2.com/g2/docs/product-information
- https://www.g2.com/categories/lease-administration
- https://www.g2.com/categories/real-estate-activities-management

## BetaList

**Verified requirements**

- Startup must be represented by an authorized submitter.
- Startup must have a working website on its own domain.
- Best fit is unreleased, private beta, or recently launched.
- Each startup can be featured once pre-launch and once during launch, with at least a few weeks between posts.
- Rejects free subdomains, app-store-only links, generic templates, low-information pages, agencies, consultancies, blogs, courses, newsletters, and old ideas without substantial improvement.
- Visitors must be able to sign up or get access.
- Editorial review is discretionary.

**CapVeri fit**

- Assessed at the time as a conditional fit, on the grounds that "CapVeri has a real domain and
  signup path". **It had neither in any reachable form.** BetaList requires a working website on
  its own domain and a way for visitors to sign up, so this was a fail, not a conditional.
- Submit only if positioning as recently launched still matches the actual launch status and prior BetaList feature history.

**Owner-only step**

- Login, confirm launch recency, confirm prior BetaList feature status, choose regular or priority review, and accept editorial changes.

**Source**

- https://betalist.com/criteria
- https://betalist.com/terms/submissions
- https://betalist.com/support

## Platform-Specific Notes

- **SaaSHub:** Include competitors in the first submission. Their guidance says missing competitors slows review.
- **AlternativeTo:** The page is alternative-driven. Lead with what CapVeri replaces or complements, not a broad SaaS pitch.
- **Product Hunt:** Avoid CRE jargon in the first sentence. Explain that landlords use CapVeri to catch CAM billing errors before tenants audit them.
- **G2:** Use category evidence, not marketing copy, because G2 category placement depends on inclusion criteria.
- **BetaList:** If CapVeri is no longer "recently launched" by their editorial judgment, do not force the fit. Keep this as a backup or paid exposure decision.
