**Long description**

```text
CapVeri is CRE FinOps software for landlord-side CAM reconciliation.

Commercial real estate teams often rely on Yardi, MRI, RealPage, AppFolio, Entrata, or spreadsheets to produce CAM statements. Those systems can calculate based on setup, but they do not always verify whether the setup matches the lease.

CapVeri imports GL, rent roll, and lease data from CSV or Excel exports, then checks the reconciliation against lease terms. It flags issues like gross-up applied to fixed expenses, wrong denominators, missing exclusions, cap tracking errors, CapEx in the recoverable pool, and weak audit evidence.

The goal is simple: help property accountants and controllers fix reconciliation issues before statements go to tenants.
```

**Who is it for?**

```text
Commercial landlords, property management firms, property controllers, property accountants, and CRE operators managing NNN or modified gross leases.
```

**What problem does it solve?**

```text
CAM reconciliation is still checked manually in spreadsheets, even when the portfolio already runs on a property management system. That leaves teams exposed to setup errors, formula mistakes, missing audit evidence, and tenant disputes. CapVeri gives those teams a verification layer that works from the files they already export.
```

**What makes it different?**

```text
CapVeri is not another property management system and does not require a Yardi or MRI integration project. It works from CSV and Excel exports, uses deterministic financial calculations, and focuses on landlord-side reconciliation verification.
```
