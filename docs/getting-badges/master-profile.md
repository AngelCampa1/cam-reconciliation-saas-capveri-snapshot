## One-Line Positioning

CapVeri verifies landlord-side CAM reconciliation before statements go out.

## Short Description

CapVeri is CRE FinOps software for commercial landlords, property managers, controllers, and property accountants. It imports GL, rent roll, and lease data from Yardi, MRI, RealPage, AppFolio, Excel, or any system that exports CSV or Excel files, then validates CAM reconciliation math against lease terms.

## Longer Description

CapVeri is the verification layer for landlord-side CAM reconciliation. Most commercial real estate teams already have a property management system, but those systems calculate based on setup and configuration. CapVeri checks whether the output is actually correct under the lease.

Upload GL exports, rent roll data, and lease terms. CapVeri runs deterministic reconciliation checks for gross-up, pro-rata share, denominators, expense caps, exclusions, CapEx classification, management fees, invoice completeness, and audit-trail evidence. Findings show the dollar impact and the lease provision behind the issue, so teams can fix statements before tenants or tenant auditors find the error.

CapVeri does not replace Yardi, MRI, RealPage, AppFolio, or Excel. It sits above them as a reconciliation and audit layer that works from files, with no ERP integration project.

## Core Jobs

- Verify CAM reconciliation before statements go out.
- Catch under-recovery, overbilling, and lease-term mismatches.
- Reduce spreadsheet dependency during reconciliation season.
- Create tenant-ready exception summaries and dispute-ready audit packets.
- Preserve an audit trail for every finding, calculation, and review decision.

## Differentiators

- Deterministic CAM math, not generated financial calculations.
- CSV and Excel import from Yardi, MRI, RealPage, AppFolio, or any PM system with a GL trial balance export.
- No API integration project, no ERP replacement, no IT dependency to start.
- Lease-term validation for gross-up, caps, pro-rata share, exclusions, denominators, and CapEx classification.
- Cumulative cap bank tracking and year-over-year trend analysis.
- Human-reviewed AI lease extraction where extraction helps, with deterministic calculation after review.
- Audit trail, dispute workflow, tenant portal, and California SB 1103 compliance exports in higher tiers.

## Claims To Avoid

- Do not claim G2 badges, Product Hunt rankings, SaaSHub awards, customer counts, press coverage, investor backing, SOC 2 status, or app store availability unless verified at submission time.
- Do not present recovered-dollar, error-count, or ROI figures as outcomes. There are none. CapVeri had no paying customer, so any such number in copy or in an image is illustrative only, and should not be submitted at all.
- Do not describe Yardi, MRI, RealPage, or AppFolio as direct integrations. CapVeri uses file imports from their exports.
- Do not say AI performs the CAM math. AI is limited to extraction and analysis workflows that require review before calculations.
