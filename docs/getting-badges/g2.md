## Product Overview, under 500 words

```text
CapVeri is CRE FinOps software for commercial landlords and property management teams that need to verify CAM reconciliation before statements go out.

Most teams already use Yardi, MRI, RealPage, AppFolio, Entrata, or Excel to produce rent rolls, GL exports, and CAM statements. CapVeri does not replace those systems. It imports the files they already produce and checks whether the reconciliation output is correct under the lease.

CapVeri validates gross-up, pro-rata share, denominators, expense caps, exclusions, CapEx classification, management fees, invoice completeness, and audit evidence. Findings show the dollar impact and the lease context behind the issue, so controllers and property accountants can fix errors before tenants receive statements.

The calculation engine is deterministic. AI is used only in reviewed extraction and analysis workflows, not as the financial calculation engine. This gives teams repeatable math, reviewable evidence, and an audit trail that supports tenant questions, internal approvals, and dispute response.

CapVeri is built for commercial real estate operators that manage NNN, modified gross, retail, office, industrial, or mixed-use portfolios and want a faster, more defensible reconciliation workflow without an ERP integration project.
```

Word count: 174.
