## Summary

A 2-3 page quick 10-question Yes/No quiz that helps property managers recognize their CAM audit exposure in under 5 minutes. Each question includes a risk interpretation for both Yes and No answers. Results guide maps Yes count to three risk bands.

## Questions

1. Do any leases give tenants audit rights?
2. Received a CAM dispute in the last 24 months?
3. Supporting documents retrievable within 48 hours?
4. Gross-up applied to variable expenses in below-90% occupancy years?
5. Cumulative CAM cap bank balances tracked year-over-year?
6. Pro-rata denominator reviewed annually or upon tenant changes?
7. Capital vs. operating expense split reviewed before sending reconciliations?
8. CAM estimates sent to California tenants 12 months in advance (SB 1103)?
9. All prior-year statements reconciled — no open years >12 months?
10. Management team reviews reconciliations before tenant delivery?

## Results Bands

| Yes Count | Band |
|-----------|------|
| 0–3 | High Risk |
| 4–6 | Moderate Risk |
| 7–10 | Well-Managed |
