## Summary

A 3-4 page self-assessment scoring tool. Property managers score 20 risk factors on a 0–5 scale (max 100 points). Risk bands: 0–39 Low, 40–69 Medium, 70–100 High. Each band includes a color-coded interpretation and recommended actions list.

## Risk Factors (20 total)

Audit-rights lease prevalence, age of oldest open reconciliation, dispute history, CAM pool structure complexity, cumulative cap tracking, gross-up consistency, management fee documentation, capital/expense split review, denominator staleness, second-reviewer process, invoice filing, Yardi/MRI recovery code review, admin fee accuracy, base year lock, exclusion checklist, statement receipt confirmation, audit window tracking, prior-year comparison practice, SB 1103 compliance, standard format consistency.
