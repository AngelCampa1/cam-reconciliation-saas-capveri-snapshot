A 2-page tenant-side resource comprising a professional cover letter template (from tenant to landlord) acknowledging receipt of a CAM reconciliation statement — with fields for company, property, suite, year, and balance — plus sections for line items under review, payment intention (pay in full or pay under protest with audit demand), and a documentation request checklist. Page 2 is a 10-item Tenant CAM Reconciliation Checklist covering pro-rata denominator, exclusions, gross-up, base year, capital items, admin and management fee caps, audit window, and prior-year trend review.
