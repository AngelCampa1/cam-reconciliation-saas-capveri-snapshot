A 5-page Triple-Net (NNN) Lease CAM Reconciliation Statement template for property managers reconciling retail, industrial, or freestanding properties where tenants pay base rent plus property taxes, insurance, and operating expenses directly. The template covers a cover page, a high-level NNN Expense Summary (budget vs. actual for all three components), a Property Tax Detail section (parcel ID, tax bill, tenant share, true-up), an Insurance Detail section (policy-by-policy), a CAM/Operating Expenses section noting standard landlord-only exclusions (leasing commissions, depreciation, corporate overhead), and a consolidated True-Up Summary with expense stop/cap calculation.
