# CapVeri YouTube Strategy: 20 Videos (PMC / Landlord)

**Audience:** Property controllers ("Patricia") and CFOs ("Charles") at property management companies and landlord-side owner-operators. Not tenants.

**Channel thesis:** This channel does not chase views. Its job is to put a PMC controller in front of a useful CAM check and the free trial at the moment their CAM reconciliation makes them nervous. The hero is the controller who wants to catch errors *before* tenants do and look sharp to their CFO. The villain is the error, the spreadsheet, the Q1 rush, and the tenant audit demand. Never the landlord.

**Whitespace:** CRE YouTube is almost entirely landlord-investing and tenant-side audit content. Landlord-side CAM *operations* content is open, so the keyword set is low-competition and high-commercial-intent at once. The highest-value operator terms carry very high CPC (lease administration software ~$133, property management accounting software ~$111, cam reconciliation software ~$63), meaning low volume but buyers worth a fortune per click.

Search volumes below are live DataForSEO US Google monthly figures captured 2026-06-02.

## Use-case legend

Each video is built to do one of four jobs:

- **S** = Search anchor (ranks on Google/YouTube, pulls cold strangers)
- **E** = Email / social (a link to drop into a campaign or post)
- **P** = Prospect answer (send it when a specific question comes up)
- **W** = Workflow guide (walks a signed-up user through a step)

## How this serves the four use cases

- **Email campaigns:** Lead nurtures with Tier 2 (7, 9, 13) plus the seasonal #20 in Q4.
- **Social:** Tier 1 clips into Shorts; post the #13 hook ("40% have an error") as a standalone.
- **Send to prospects with questions:** #16 to the IT blocker, #5 to the "why not just keep my spreadsheet" objection, #19 to the security-review concern.
- **Guide through workflows:** #2, #14, and #18 live inside onboarding and the help center.
