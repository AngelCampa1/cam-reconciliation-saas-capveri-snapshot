## 9. camaudit.io — AI-SEO Observations

1. **Overwhelming AIO presence (>97%):** Only 2 of 103 keyword entries lack an AI Overview (`nnn cam`, `nnn calculator`). Every CAM-related topic is now an AI-answerable query. camaudit.io content is cited across the board — this is both validation and a risk (AIO suppresses organic CTR).
2. **Zero featured snippets across all 78 keywords.** This is the single largest quick-win gap. Structured content (short definition paragraphs, ordered lists for processes, markdown tables for comparisons) with `speakable` and FAQ schema would target the snippet position that AIO doesn't always occupy.
3. **Deep page-2/3 ranking for high-volume head terms:** "cam charges" (vol 3,600, rank 68), "cam charge" (vol 2,900, rank 72), "what are cam charges" (vol 1,000, rank 72) are all on page 7–8 despite the site's authoritative content. This indicates a domain authority / backlink gap vs. established competitors, not a content quality issue.
4. **Glossary strength with low KD:** Multiple glossary terms rank for KD 1–8 keywords (reserves fund KD 3, rate of absorption KD 2, special assessments KD 1) — these are prime featured-snippet targets with minimal competition.
5. **Minimal PAA ownership:** PAA boxes appear on almost all SERPs but camaudit.io does not seem to be surfaced as a PAA source (all are Y meaning PAA box exists, not that camaudit.io ranks in it). Creating dedicated Q&A-format pages or adding FAQ schema will target PAA entry.
6. **Tools section underperforming:** /tools ranks at position 25 for "cam audit software" (vol 390) and position 84 for "cam calculation formula" (vol 70). The tools page needs clearer heading hierarchy and dedicated tool-specific landing pages.
